## 1. Why this exists

Palencia Diamonds sells trust. We handle high-value stones, personal customer data, and one of the most emotional purchases in a person's life. A single dishonest act — a shaded grade, a self-dealt stone, a leaked customer record — can undo years of earned reputation.

This Code turns our [values](../00-company/mission-vision-values.md) into concrete rules of behavior. It applies to **everyone**: employees, contractors, temporary staff, and anyone acting on Palencia's behalf.

> This is a policy document, not legal advice. Where the Code touches law (AML/KYC, privacy, employment), qualified counsel and local law govern. See [CONTRIBUTING §5](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

## 2. Our standard, in one line

> **If we wouldn't say it or do it in front of the customer buying their engagement ring, we don't say it or do it.**

Every rule below is an application of that standard.

## 3. Integrity & honesty

Honesty is the product. That is not a slogan here — it is the job.

- **Never misrepresent a stone or a price.** No overstating a grade, hiding an inclusion, inventing a "was" price, or implying certification we don't have.
- **Every center stone ships with its genuine, matching GIA/IGI report.** We never substitute, alter, or misattribute a certificate.
- **We describe treatments, origins, and lab-grown vs. natural accurately, every time.** Silence that misleads is a lie.
- **Honor our promises:** free insured shipping, resizing where design allows, and manufacturing-defect coverage — as stated, not as reinterpreted to avoid a cost.
- **If we make a mistake, we say so** — to the customer and internally — and we fix it.

---

## 4. Treatment of customers

- **No pressure, ever.** We inform and guide; the customer decides in their own time.
- **Meet customers where they are.** Explain the 4Cs and trade-offs plainly, without jargon used to intimidate.
- **Resolve complaints generously and fast.** Treat every review — good or bad — as a signal to improve.
- **Never exploit vulnerability or emotion** to upsell. The moment belongs to the customer, not to our commission.
- **Accessibility and respect** for every customer regardless of budget or background.

## 5. Handling high-value inventory

Stones and finished pieces are the company's — and our customers' — property in our care. Treat them accordingly.

### 5.4 Security

- Follow all inventory-control, storage, and transit procedures. See [Quality Control](../03-operations/quality-control.md) and Operations SOPs. `TODO: [link to inventory-security SOP]`.
- **Two-person handling and sign-off** for high-value stone movements wherever staffing allows.
- Never remove stones or pieces from the premises without documented authorization.
- Every movement is logged: who, what (stone ID / certificate #), when, where.
- Report any discrepancy, loss, or suspected theft **immediately** to the Operations Manager and Finance & Compliance.

### 5.5 No self-dealing

This is a bright line. Violations are grounds for termination and, where applicable, referral to authorities.

- **You may not buy company inventory for yourself, family, or friends at non-standard terms**, or route a good stone/deal to yourself instead of the company.
- **The person who buys a stone may not be the sole person who values it** for pricing. The person who authorizes a refund may not be the sole person who issues it. See [Organizational Structure §7](organizational-structure.md#7-a-lean-org-dual-hatting--separation-of-duties).
- **No personal side-dealing in diamonds or jewelry** that competes with, or uses information/relationships from, Palencia. Disclose any existing trade activity to Finance & Compliance.

## 6. Conflicts of interest

A conflict is any situation where personal interest could compromise a decision made for Palencia.

- **Disclose, don't hide.** If in doubt, disclose it to your manager and Finance & Compliance.
- **Common conflicts to disclose:** financial interest in a supplier or competitor; a close relationship with a supplier, customer, or job candidate; outside work in jewelry, gemology, or diamonds; family in the trade.
- **Recuse** yourself from any decision (sourcing, pricing, hiring, refunds) where you have a conflict.
- A disclosed, managed conflict is fine; an undisclosed one is a violation.

## 7. Confidentiality & data protection

- **Customer data is sacred.** Names, addresses, purchase and financial details, and the fact that someone is buying a ring (often a surprise) are strictly confidential. Access only what you need to do your job.
- **Never disclose a customer's purchase** to anyone, including a partner or family member, without the customer's authorization.
- **Protect business-confidential information:** supplier terms, pricing formulas, margins, designs, and strategy.

## 9. Anti-bribery, AML & responsible sourcing

As a dealer in precious stones and jewels, we carry legal obligations that individual conduct must support.

- **No bribes or kickbacks**, offered or accepted, in any form, anywhere.
- **Support the AML/KYC program.** Follow customer-identification, large/cash-transaction, and suspicious-activity procedures. Never help a customer structure payments to evade reporting. Owner: Finance & Compliance (FinCEN obligations for dealers). `TODO: [link to AML/KYC SOP]`.
- **Source responsibly.** Uphold the Kimberley Process, System of Warranties, OECD Due Diligence Guidance, and RJC-aligned practices. Never knowingly handle conflict or undocumented stones. See [Responsible Sourcing](../05-compliance-ethics/responsible-sourcing.md).
- **Escalate red flags immediately** to Finance & Compliance — an unusual payment, a supplier who can't document origin, a customer avoiding identification.

## 11. Reporting violations & whistleblower protection

**Speak up. It is always safe to do the right thing here.**

### 11.4 How to report

| Channel | Use when |
|---|---|
| Your manager | Most concerns, first stop. |
| People / HR Lead | Workplace conduct, harassment, discrimination. |
| Finance & Compliance Officer | Fraud, self-dealing, AML/sourcing, financial integrity. |
| CEO / Founder | The above roles are involved or unavailable. |
| Confidential/anonymous channel | You prefer anonymity. `TODO: [hotline / web form / third-party service]` |

Reports can be made in good faith without proof; you do not need to be certain, only honest.

### 11.5 Our promise to reporters

- **No retaliation.** Retaliating against someone who reports a concern in good faith is itself a serious violation.
- **Confidentiality** to the fullest extent possible consistent with a fair investigation.
- **We take every report seriously**, investigate fairly and promptly, and close the loop with the reporter where we can.
- Knowingly false or malicious reports are the only exception and may themselves be addressed.

## 12. Consequences

Violations are handled fairly, proportionately, and consistently:

| Severity | Examples | Typical response |
|---|---|---|
| Minor / first-time | Procedural lapse, missed disclosure corrected promptly | Coaching, retraining, documented conversation |
| Serious | Repeated lapses, undisclosed conflict, negligent handling of inventory/data | Formal warning, role/access changes, performance plan |
| Severe | Self-dealing, theft, misrepresenting a stone/price, harassment, AML/sourcing violation, retaliation | Termination and, where warranted, referral to authorities |

Consequences follow due process under applicable employment law and are owned by People/Culture with Finance & Compliance and the CEO. See [Decision-Making §6](decision-making.md#6-escalation-paths).
