## 1. Purpose & scope

This policy governs how we source, verify, and stand behind the origin and integrity of:

- **Natural diamonds** (loose and mounted center stones and melee)
- **Laboratory-grown diamonds**
- **Colored gemstones** `[TODO: confirm whether Palencia stocks colored stones; scope in if so]`
- **Precious metals** — gold (yellow, white, rose) and platinum

It applies to every employee, supplier, contractor, and refiner in our supply chain, and to every purchase order, memo, and consignment.

**Our commitment:** We will only sell what we can stand behind ethically. If a stone or material cannot be traced and warranted to our standard, we do not buy it.

## 2. The frameworks we operate against

We align our program to the recognized international standards for the diamond and jewelry trade. None of these is optional in spirit; several carry the force of law in the jurisdictions we serve.

| Framework | What it is | How we apply it |
|---|---|---|
| **Kimberley Process Certification Scheme (KPCS)** | Intergovernmental scheme (launched 2003) preventing "conflict diamonds" — rough diamonds used by rebel movements to finance war against legitimate governments — from entering the legitimate rough trade. Requires KP certificates for cross-border rough shipments. | We buy only from suppliers who participate in the KPCS and can evidence that rough entered the pipeline through KP-certified channels. |
| **World Diamond Council (WDC) System of Warranties (SoW)** | Industry self-regulation that extends KPCS assurance beyond rough to polished diamonds and jewelry, via a written warranty passed on every invoice. | We collect an SoW warranty on every inbound invoice and pass one on every outbound invoice. See §5. |
| **OECD Due Diligence Guidance for Responsible Supply Chains of Minerals from Conflict-Affected and High-Risk Areas (CAHRAs)** | The global standard for risk-based supply-chain due diligence, built on a **five-step framework**. Applies to diamonds, gold, and other minerals. | Our due-diligence program (§4) is structured on the OECD five steps. |
| **Responsible Jewellery Council (RJC) Code of Practices (CoP)** | Certifiable standard covering responsible business, human rights, labor, environment, product disclosure, and chain-of-custody across the jewelry supply chain. | We align our practices to the CoP and prioritize RJC-certified suppliers. `[TODO: confirm whether Palencia is pursuing RJC membership/certification]` |
| **UN Guiding Principles on Business & Human Rights (UNGPs)** | The authoritative framework for the corporate responsibility to respect human rights. | Underpins our grievance mechanism (§8) and human-rights expectations of suppliers. |
| **Applicable sanctions & import law** | E.g., US OFAC sanctions and diamond import restrictions; G7 measures on Russian-origin diamonds. | Screened under [AML & KYC](aml-kyc.md) and at the point of purchase. `[TODO: confirm current Russian-diamond import/traceability obligations for our markets with counsel]` |

## 3. Our sourcing principles

1. **Traceability first.** We prefer suppliers who can evidence origin and chain of custody, not merely assert it.
2. **Documented, not assumed.** Every claim (conflict-free, origin, karatage, lab-grown vs. natural) must be supported by a document we retain.
3. **Risk-based effort.** We apply deeper diligence to higher-risk countries, materials, and counterparties (see §4).
4. **No willful blindness.** Red flags are investigated, not ignored, and unresolved red flags stop the purchase.
5. **Continuous improvement.** We re-evaluate suppliers and update this policy as frameworks evolve.

## 4. Supplier due diligence — the OECD five steps

We implement due diligence on a **risk-based** basis, structured on the OECD Guidance.

| Step | OECD requirement | What Palencia does |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Strong management systems** | Adopt a supply-chain policy; assign responsibility; keep records; strengthen engagement with suppliers. | This policy; the Compliance Officer owns it; supplier records retained per §7; SoW clauses in supplier terms. |
| **2. Identify & assess risk** | Map the supply chain and assess risk in the chain of custody. | Supplier onboarding questionnaire; KP participation check; beneficial-ownership and sanctions screening via [AML & KYC](aml-kyc.md); country-of-origin risk rating. |
| **3. Design & implement a response** | Report to senior management; adopt a risk-management plan; monitor. | Risk-tiered supplier classification (below); mitigation or disengagement for high risk; escalation to management. |
| **4. Independent third-party audit** | Audit due diligence at identified points in the chain. | Prefer suppliers with third-party/RJC audits; `[TODO: define whether/when Palencia commissions independent audits]` |
| **5. Report** | Publicly report on supply-chain due diligence. | `[TODO: decide cadence and venue for a public responsible-sourcing statement, e.g., annual statement on palenciadiamonds.com]` |

### Supplier risk tiers

| Tier | Criteria | Diligence applied |
|---|---|---|
| **Low** | RJC-certified; long track record; low-risk jurisdiction; full documentation. | Standard onboarding + annual warranty refresh. |
| **Medium** | Newer relationship; complete but unaudited documentation. | Enhanced questionnaire; sample document verification; sanctions re-screen. |
| **High** | Any exposure to a CAHRA; incomplete documentation; adverse media; ownership opacity. | Enhanced due diligence, senior sign-off, or **do not onboard**. Escalate to Compliance Officer. |

### Supplier onboarding checklist

- [ ] Legal entity, beneficial ownership, and jurisdiction captured
- [ ] Sanctions / OFAC / PEP screening cleared (see [AML & KYC](aml-kyc.md))
- [ ] Evidence of KPCS participation for rough-derived goods
- [ ] Signed acceptance of our supplier code / responsible-sourcing terms
- [ ] System of Warranties statement present on invoices
- [ ] RJC certification status recorded (if any)
- [ ] Lab-grown vs. natural disclosure practices confirmed
- [ ] Country-of-origin and traceability claims documented
- [ ] Risk tier assigned and recorded

## 5. The System of Warranties (SoW)

Every diamond invoice — inbound and outbound — must carry the WDC System of Warranties statement. We **collect** it from suppliers and **pass** it to customers and trade buyers.

### Standard warranty statement (natural diamonds)

> "The diamonds herein invoiced have been sourced from legitimate sources not involved in funding conflict, in compliance with United Nations Resolutions and the corresponding national laws. The seller hereby guarantees that these diamonds are conflict-free and confirms adherence to the World Diamond Council System of Warranties."

> [!NOTE]
> The WDC updated the SoW warranty language to reference broader human- and labor-rights and anti-money-laundering principles. Use the **current WDC-published wording** on our invoices. `[TODO: Compliance Officer to confirm and lock the exact current SoW statement text with counsel and update this doc.]`

### Rules

- **No warranty, no goods.** A diamond invoice without a valid SoW statement is not accepted into inventory.
- We do not "flow through" a warranty we did not receive — we escalate and withhold the goods until it is provided.
- Outbound: our customer-facing invoices and trade invoices include the passed-on warranty.
- Warranties are retained per §7 recordkeeping.

## 6. Lab-grown vs. natural — disclosure & handling

Laboratory-grown diamonds are real diamonds; they are **not** substitutes to be blurred with natural stones. Honest, unambiguous disclosure is mandatory and aligns with the [Ethics Policy](ethics-policy.md) and FTC Jewelry Guides.

| Requirement | Standard |
|---|---|
| **Terminology** | Disclose lab-grown stones as "laboratory-grown," "lab-created," or "[manufacturer]-created." Never imply a lab-grown stone is natural, and never use "synthetic" in a way that misleads. |
| **Placement** | Disclosure appears on the product page, the invoice, the grading report reference, and any appraisal. |
| **Grading** | Lab-grown center stones ship with an independent report (e.g., IGI or GIA laboratory-grown report). See [Certification & Grading](certification-and-grading.md). |
| **Inscription** | Prefer stones with a laser inscription noting laboratory-grown origin and report number. |
| **Segregation** | Natural and lab-grown inventory are physically and systemically segregated to prevent commingling or mislabeling. |
| **Kimberley Process** | KPCS applies to **natural rough**; lab-grown is out of KPCS scope but remains within our responsible-sourcing, energy-origin, and honest-disclosure expectations. `[TODO: capture lab-grown producer sustainability/energy claims we are willing to make]` |

## 7. Responsible precious metals

Gold and platinum carry their own supply-chain risks (conflict gold, artisanal-mining harms, laundering). Our metals sourcing aligns to the same risk-based approach.

|---|---|

| **LBMA Responsible Gold / Responsible Sourcing** | Refiner-level assurance; we prefer metal from LBMA Good Delivery / responsibly-audited refiners. |
| **RJC Chain-of-Custody (CoC)** | Segregated, audited responsible metal where available. |

## 8. Disengagement & red-flag response

Not every risk can be mitigated. Where it can't, we stop.

| Trigger | Response |
|---|---|
| Missing or unverifiable SoW warranty | Withhold goods until provided; do not accept into inventory |
| Credible evidence of conflict financing | Suspend purchases; escalate to Compliance Officer and counsel |
| Sanctions match on counterparty | Halt immediately; do not ship or pay; escalate (see [AML & KYC](aml-kyc.md)) |
| Serious human-rights/labor findings | Require remediation plan; disengage if unresolved |
| Repeated documentation failures | Move supplier to high-risk tier or offboard |
| Refusal to accept our sourcing terms | Do not onboard |

Disengagement decisions and their rationale are documented and retained.
