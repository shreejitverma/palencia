> [!IMPORTANT]
> **This document is operational guidance, not legal advice.** Advertising, anti-bribery, competition, and whistleblower laws differ by jurisdiction and change over time. It must be reviewed by qualified counsel for every jurisdiction in which Palencia Diamonds operates before being treated as binding. Where law and this policy conflict, the law governs.

## 1. Why this policy exists

"Honesty is the product" is our first value. In a certified-diamond business, integrity is not a compliance box — it is the brand. This policy translates that value into concrete rules for how we advertise, sell, buy, compete, and treat one another. It applies to **every employee, officer, contractor, and agent** of Palencia Diamonds.

## 2. Honest advertising & no deceptive grading claims

We describe what we sell exactly as it is. Our advertising, product pages, and sales conversations align with the **FTC Guides for the Jewelry, Precious Metals, and Pewter Industries ("Jewelry Guides")** and equivalent standards in other markets.

| Topic | Standard |
|---|---|
| **Lab-grown diamonds** | Always disclosed as "laboratory-grown," "lab-created," or "[maker]-created." Never implied to be natural. See [Certification & Grading](certification-and-grading.md). |
| **Natural diamonds** | Called "diamond" without qualifier only when natural and untreated (or with disclosed treatment). |
| **Simulants** | Moissanite, CZ, and other non-diamond materials are never called "diamond." |
| **Treatments** | Any treatment (clarity enhancement, HPHT, fracture filling, laser drilling, etc.) is disclosed clearly and near the product claim. |
| **Grading claims** | We describe cut, color, clarity, and carat exactly as independently graded — never upgraded in copy. Carat stated to the hundredth; never rounded up. |
| **Gold karatage** | Karat (e.g., 14K, 18K) stated accurately; underkarating is prohibited. |
| **Plating / fill** | "Gold-plated," "gold-filled," and "vermeil" used precisely and never as "gold." |
| **Platinum** | Described per applicable purity/stamping standards. |
| **Pricing & savings** | "Honest prices" — no fictitious "was" prices, fake urgency, or misleading discounts. |
| **Origin & sourcing** | Conflict-free and responsible-sourcing claims are backed by warranties and diligence (see [Responsible Sourcing](responsible-sourcing.md)). |

**Rule:** If a claim can't be substantiated by a grading report, a hallmark, or documented diligence, we don't make it.

## 3. Anti-bribery & anti-corruption

We do not offer, give, solicit, or accept bribes or improper payments — ever. We follow the principles of the **US Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA)** and the **UK Bribery Act 2010** (which reaches commercial bribery and failure to prevent bribery), and applicable local law.

| Prohibited | Detail |
|---|---|
| Bribes / kickbacks | No payment or benefit to improperly influence a customer, supplier, or official. |
| Facilitation payments | Discouraged/prohibited; the UK Bribery Act does not exempt them. `[TODO: confirm position with counsel.]` |
| Improper supplier inducements | No paying (or accepting) kickbacks to win or place business. |
| Third-party conduits | We are liable for agents/intermediaries who bribe on our behalf; diligence required. |
| False records | All payments accurately recorded — no off-book funds (ties to [Financial Controls](../08-finance/financial-controls.md)). |

### Corruption red flags (watch for these)

- [ ] A supplier or agent requests unusual commissions, "consulting fees," or cash
- [ ] Pressure to route payments to a third party, offshore account, or shell entity
- [ ] Requests to falsify invoices, descriptions, or values
- [ ] Reluctance to put terms in writing or accept anti-bribery clauses
- [ ] Gifts or hospitality offered around a live sourcing decision
- [ ] An intermediary with no clear role demanding a cut

Any of these goes to the Compliance Officer before proceeding.

## 5. Conflicts of interest

A conflict of interest arises when personal interests could improperly influence business judgment.

| Situation | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Financial interest in a supplier/competitor | Disclose to Compliance Officer; recuse from related decisions |
| Personal relationship with a counterparty | Disclose; avoid self-dealing |
| Outside business/side dealings in stones or jewelry | Disclose; must not compete with or exploit Palencia |
| Hiring or supervising relatives | Disclose; no sole decision authority over their role |
| Personal purchases / employee discount abuse | Follow policy; no reselling for personal profit against Palencia's interest |

**Rule:** Disclose early. A disclosed conflict can be managed; a hidden one is a violation.

## 9. Applying the policy — worked scenarios

Ethics rules are easiest to follow when they're concrete. These illustrate the standard.

| Scenario | Wrong | Right |
|---|---|---|
| A lab-grown stone photographs beautifully; marketing wants a "diamond" headline | Call it a "diamond" and bury the origin | Headline discloses "lab-grown diamond"; origin repeated on page and invoice |
| A supplier offers concert tickets during price negotiation | Accept quietly | Decline; log the offer with the Compliance Officer |
| A customer wants to pay $14,000 cash split across two days | Accommodate to "help" them | Follow [AML & KYC](aml-kyc.md); refuse structuring; escalate |
| An SI2 stone is described internally as "eye-clean VS" to move it | Upgrade the grade in copy | Describe exactly as graded; let the price reflect the grade |
| An employee's cousin runs a casting shop and wants our business | Award work quietly | Disclose the relationship; recuse from the decision |
| A "was $5,000, now $2,500" banner with no real prior price | Run it for urgency | Use only truthful reference prices |
