> **This document is operational guidance, not legal advice.** Grading nomenclature, disclosure duties, and appraisal/insurance requirements interact with consumer-protection law (e.g., FTC Jewelry Guides) and vary by jurisdiction. It must be reviewed by qualified counsel for the jurisdictions in which Palencia operates before being treated as binding for advertising or contractual claims.

## 1. Purpose

This document sets the standard for how we certify, describe, verify, and disclose the diamonds we sell. It is the reference for merchandising, quality control, sales, and marketing. Our promise is simple: **independent certification, always — not our word for it.**

## 2. What certification is (and isn't)

- A **grading report** is an expert, independent laboratory's assessment of a diamond's characteristics (the 4Cs plus additional detail). It is **not** a valuation and **not** a guarantee of price.
- An **appraisal** is a separate document estimating monetary value for insurance or resale (see §9).
- "Certified" in trade usage means the stone has an independent laboratory grading report. We use the term only when a genuine, verifiable report exists.

## 3. GIA vs. IGI — the labs we use

Every Palencia center stone ships with an independent report from **GIA** or **IGI**.

| | **GIA — Gemological Institute of America** | **IGI — International Gemological Institute** |
|---|---|---|
| Founded / nature | Nonprofit research & education institute; creator of the modern 4Cs and the D-to-Z color and Flawless-to-I3 clarity scales. | Independent commercial gemological laboratory with a global network. |
| Reputation | Widely regarded as the benchmark for natural-diamond grading consistency. | Widely used, especially prominent for **laboratory-grown** diamond reports and jewelry reports. |
| Common use at Palencia | Natural center stones, especially higher-value. | Both natural and laboratory-grown stones; volume grading. |
| Report verification | Online **GIA Report Check**. | Online **IGI Report Verification**. |

> [!NOTE]
> Grading is expert opinion, and standards can differ subtly between laboratories. We do not mix-and-match a report from one lab with claims calibrated to another, and we disclose which laboratory graded each stone.

## 4. What a grading report contains

| Section | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Report number & date | Unique identifier used for verification |
| Shape & cutting style | e.g., round brilliant, princess, oval, emerald |
| Measurements | Dimensions in mm |
| **Carat weight** | To the hundredth of a carat |
| **Color grade** | D–Z scale (or "fancy" color descriptor for colored diamonds) |
| **Clarity grade** | FL–I3 scale |
| **Cut grade** | For standard round brilliants (Excellent–Poor); proportions for fancy shapes |
| Polish & symmetry | Excellent–Poor |
| Fluorescence | None–Very Strong (see §6) |
| Proportions / plotting | Diagram of inclusions and proportions |
| Laser inscription | Report number (and origin/type) inscribed on the girdle, if present |
| Origin/type statement | Natural vs. **laboratory-grown**; any detected treatments |

## 5. The 4Cs — grading scales

See [Diamond Education: The 4Cs](../09-product/diamond-education-4cs.md) for the customer-facing explanation. The scales below are the standard we grade and describe against.

### Cut (for round brilliants)

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Excellent | Maximizes brilliance and fire |
| Very Good | Near-excellent light performance |
| Good | Balanced, value-oriented |
| Fair / Poor | Reduced light performance |

> Cut is the C most responsible for a diamond's visible sparkle. Fancy shapes are described by proportion, not a single GIA cut grade.

### Color (D–Z)

| Range | Description |
|---|---|
| **D–F** | Colorless |
| **G–J** | Near-colorless |
| **K–M** | Faint |
| **N–R** | Very light |
| **S–Z** | Light |

Beyond Z, diamonds are graded as **Fancy** colored diamonds on a separate scale.

### Clarity (FL–I3)

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **FL / IF** | Flawless / Internally Flawless |
| **VVS1 / VVS2** | Very, Very Slightly Included |
| **VS1 / VS2** | Very Slightly Included |
| **SI1 / SI2** | Slightly Included |
| **I1 / I2 / I3** | Included (visible characteristics) |

### Carat

- Weight measured in carats (1 carat = 0.2 grams = 100 points).
- We state carat weight **to the hundredth** and never round up in customer-facing descriptions.

## 6. Fluorescence

- Fluorescence is a diamond's tendency to glow (usually blue) under UV light, graded **None, Faint, Medium, Strong, Very Strong**.
- It is a characteristic, not a flaw; strong fluorescence can slightly affect appearance in some stones. We disclose the fluorescence grade and never hide it.

## 7. Laser inscription

- Many graded stones carry a **laser inscription** on the girdle — typically the report number, and for lab-grown, an origin note.
- Where present, inscription lets us and the customer match the physical stone to its report. QC records the inscription at intake.

## 8. Verifying report authenticity

Every report is verified against the issuing laboratory's official tool before a stone enters inventory and again if a report is ever in doubt.

### Verification checklist

- [ ] Confirm report number against the physical report and inscription
- [ ] Verify on the official lab tool (**GIA Report Check** / **IGI Report Verification**)
- [ ] Confirm the online record matches carat, color, clarity, cut, and shape on hand
- [ ] Confirm natural vs. laboratory-grown status matches our listing
- [ ] Flag any mismatch to QC and the Compliance Officer; **quarantine the stone** until resolved
- [ ] Record verification date and result in the item file

> A stone whose report cannot be verified does not ship. No exceptions.

## 10. Disclosure standards — treatments & lab-grown

Accurate disclosure is mandatory and aligns with the [Ethics Policy](ethics-policy.md) and FTC Jewelry Guides.

| Item | Standard |
|---|---|
| **Laboratory-grown** | Clearly labeled "laboratory-grown"/"lab-created"/"[maker]-created" on the product page, invoice, and report reference. Never implied to be natural. |
| **Treatments** | Any treatment (e.g., clarity enhancement, HPHT color treatment, fracture filling, laser drilling) is disclosed prominently. Untreated is the default claim only when confirmed. |
| **Simulants** | Non-diamond simulants (e.g., moissanite, CZ) are never described as "diamond." |
| **Report source** | The grading laboratory (GIA/IGI) is named for each center stone. |

## 11. Our policy commitments

1. **Every center stone ships with an independent GIA or IGI report.**
2. **Every report is verified** on the official lab tool before sale.
3. **We describe stones exactly as graded** — no upgrading of color, clarity, or carat in copy.
4. **Lab-grown and treatments are disclosed** unambiguously, everywhere.
5. **Mismatches quarantine the stone** and escalate to QC and Compliance.

## 12. Intake-to-listing procedure

Every certified stone moves through the same controlled path before it can be sold.

| Step | Action | Owner | Record |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Receive stone with its grading report | Merchandising | PO / intake log |
| 2 | Match physical inscription to report number | QC | Item file |
| 3 | Verify report on the official lab tool (§8) | QC | Verification date/result |
| 4 | Confirm 4Cs, shape, and natural/lab-grown status match | QC | Item file |
| 5 | Confirm SoW warranty present on invoice (natural) | Compliance | See [Responsible Sourcing](responsible-sourcing.md) |
| 6 | Create listing mirroring the report exactly | Merchandising | Product page |
| 7 | Attach report reference + disclosures to listing/invoice | Merchandising | Product page / invoice |
| 8 | Any mismatch → quarantine + escalate | QC / Compliance | Exception log |

> A stone cannot be listed until steps 1-7 are complete and clean.

## 13. Common questions (for sales & CX)

| Question | Honest answer to give |
|---|---|
| "Is a GIA stone better than an IGI stone?" | Both are independent labs; GIA is the benchmark for natural grading, IGI is widely used and strong for lab-grown. We name the lab for every stone. |
| "Is a lab-grown diamond a 'real' diamond?" | Yes — it's chemically and optically diamond, grown in a lab rather than the earth. We always label it lab-grown. |
| "Can I check the report myself?" | Yes — use the lab's official Report Check / Verification tool with the report number. |
| "Does fluorescence mean it's a worse diamond?" | Not inherently; it's a characteristic we disclose. It can slightly affect appearance in some stones. |
