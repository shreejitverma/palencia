## 1. Welcome

Welcome to Palencia Diamonds. You've joined a company built on one uncompromising idea: **certified diamonds, handcrafted jewelry, and honest prices — for everyone, not just the few.**

We sell the pieces that mark life's biggest moments — engagements, weddings, anniversaries, milestones. That means the work you do here reaches people on the best days of their lives. It also means the bar is high: a wrong carat weight, a misquoted grade, a late ship date, or a careless word can break a trust that took years to build.

This handbook explains how we work, what you can expect from us, and what we expect from you. Read it once end to end, then keep it handy. For the "why" behind all of it, start with the [Company Overview](../00-company/company-overview.md) and [Mission, Vision & Values](../00-company/mission-vision-values.md).

## 2. Our culture and values in daily practice

Our six values aren't posters — they're how we make decisions. Here's what each looks like in the work.

| Value | What it means at your desk / bench |
|---|---|
| **Honesty is the product** | Tell the customer the truth about a grade, a price, a trade-off, or a timeline. If you wouldn't say it to a close friend buying their ring, don't say it to a customer. Never oversell a grade or hide a flaw. |
| **Craftsmanship over shortcuts** | Every piece meets a standard we'd be proud to wear. We'd rather delay a piece than ship one that isn't right. No exceptions for deadlines. |
| **The customer's moment, not our transaction** | Patience, care, zero pressure. We're a small part of one of the biggest moments in someone's life — we act like it. |
| **Do right by the earth and its people** | We only handle and sell what we can stand behind ethically. Responsible, conflict-free sourcing is a requirement, not a slogan. |
| **Earn trust, then earn it again** | Every employee is a steward of the Palencia name. Reputation is built one honest interaction at a time. |
| **Precision in everything** | Check twice, document everything, follow the SOPs. Accuracy is a discipline, not a talent. |

When a decision is hard, the values break the tie: **honesty and the customer's moment come first.**

## 7. Security and handling high-value inventory

This is the section that most sets Palencia apart from an ordinary workplace. We hold diamonds, finished jewelry, precious metals, and the certificates that give them value. Losses here are not just financial — they break customer trust and can trigger insurance and regulatory consequences.

**Everyone who touches physical goods must follow these rules. No exceptions.**

- [ ] **Chain of custody.** Every stone and piece is logged in and out. Never move inventory without recording it in `[bracketed] TODO: inventory system` per [Inventory Management](../03-operations/inventory-management.md).
- [ ] **Dual control for high-value items.** `[bracketed] TODO: Define the value threshold` requires two authorized people for handling, counting, and vault access.
- [ ] **Secured areas only.** Loose stones and finished high-value pieces stay in designated secured zones and safes/vaults. Never in a pocket, bag, drawer, car, or home.
- [ ] **Certificates stay with stones.** GIA/IGI reports are matched to their stone by report number at every handoff. A mismatch is a stop-work event — report it immediately.
- [ ] **Access control.** Badge/keys/vault codes are personal and non-transferable. Never share credentials or prop open a secured door.
- [ ] **Reconciliation.** Physical counts reconcile to the system on the `[bracketed] TODO: cadence`. Discrepancies are escalated the same day.
- [ ] **Visitors & couriers.** Escort all visitors in secured areas. Verify courier identity before any handoff; follow the [Shipping & Insurance](../03-operations/shipping-and-insurance.md) procedure for insured, signature-confirmed movement.
- [ ] **No solo after-hours handling** of high-value inventory without written authorization.
- [ ] **Report loss, theft, or discrepancy immediately** to `[bracketed] TODO: security/loss-prevention contact` — hiding or delaying a report is a serious conduct violation.

Suspicious transactions or customer behavior may also trigger AML obligations — see the [AML & KYC](../05-compliance-ethics/aml-kyc.md) policy and escalate to the compliance owner.

- **Customer privacy.** Handle personal data strictly per [Data Privacy & Security](../05-compliance-ethics/data-privacy-and-security.md) and applicable privacy law. Never discuss a customer's purchase publicly — these are private, emotional milestones.

- Keep hands and workstations clean; oils and debris damage stones and finishes.
