## 1. Our approach

In a certified-diamond business, **expertise is the difference between a trusted advisor and a salesperson.** A customer can tell within minutes whether the person guiding them actually understands cut, or is reciting a script. So we invest in real, credentialed skill — and we make growth a defined path, not a lucky accident.

Three principles:
1. **Credible over cursory.** Where recognized industry credentials exist (GIA, IGI), we pursue them.
2. **Practice beats slides.** Grading, setting, and consultation are learned by doing under a mentor.
3. **Compliance is non-negotiable.** Certain training is required to hold the role — no completion, no access.

## 2. Gemology and diamond grading

The foundation. Depth scales with role, but everyone customer-facing learns the language of the 4Cs — see [Diamond Education: The 4Cs](../09-product/diamond-education-4cs.md).

| Track | Audience | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **4Cs fundamentals** | All customer-facing & product staff | Cut, color, clarity, carat; how to read a GIA/IGI report; fluorescence, proportions, and what actually drives beauty and price. |
| **Applied grading** | Gemology, QC, senior sales | Hands-on grading against known stones; reconciling observation to the lab report; identifying treatments and simulants. |
| **GIA / IGI coursework** | Gemology track; growth path for others | Structured external courses (see §3). |
| **Lab-grown vs. natural** | All product & sales | Honest, accurate explanation of differences, disclosure, and pricing — central to "honesty is the product." |

## 3. The GG credential as a growth path

The **GIA Graduate Gemologist (GG)** is the industry's gold standard, and Palencia treats it as a marquee growth path. We also recognize stepping-stone credentials.

| Credential | Level | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|
| **GIA AJP (Applied Jewelry Professional)** | Entry | New sales/CX staff — a strong first credential. |
| **GIA Diamonds / Diamond Grading** | Intermediate | Sales, QC, aspiring gemologists. |
| **GIA Graduate Gemologist (GG)** | Advanced | Gemology track; senior QC and merchandising. |
| **IGI programs** | Varies | Alternative/complementary coursework. |

## 4. Product knowledge

Beyond stones: metals, settings, and the catalog itself.

- **Materials & metals** — gold (yellow/white/rose) and platinum, durability, care, hallmarks. See [Materials & Metals](../09-product/materials-and-metals.md).
- **Product taxonomy** — how the catalog is organized so staff can navigate and advise. See [Product Taxonomy](../09-product/product-taxonomy.md).
- **Settings & construction** — solitaire, halo, three-stone, pavé, eternity; how construction affects durability, resizing, and price.
- **Custom design** — how a bespoke commission goes from idea to finished ring. See [Custom Design Workflow](../03-operations/custom-design-workflow.md).

## 5. Consultative sales and customer experience

We don't "close" — we guide. Training reinforces patience, honesty, and zero pressure.

| Module | Focus |
|---|---|
| **The Palencia consultation** | Discovery, educating without overwhelming, matching budget to the right piece honestly. See [Sales Playbook](../04-sales-cx/sales-playbook.md). |
| **Emotional intelligence** | Reading nervous first-time buyers; holding space for a huge personal moment. |
| **Honest trade-off coaching** | How to explain why a slightly smaller, better-cut stone may beat a bigger, duller one — even when the bigger one costs more. |
| **Service standards** | Response times, tone, and follow-through. See [Customer Experience Standards](../04-sales-cx/customer-experience-standards.md). |
| **Returns, warranty & resizing** | Communicating our guarantees generously and accurately. |
| **Objection handling with integrity** | Never pressure; answer the real question. |

## 7. Craft apprenticeship for setters and jewelers

Bench mastery is earned at the bench, over years, under a master. We run a structured apprenticeship.

| Stage | Focus | Sign-off |
|---|---|---|
| **Apprentice** | Basics: metal prep, soldering, polishing, tool care; practice on scrap/CZ only — never client stones. | Mentor confirms fundamentals |
| **Junior setter** | Prong, bezel, and channel setting on lower-risk pieces under review; all work QC-inspected. | QC + mentor sign-off |
| **Setter** | Independent setting across styles to standard; pavé and micro-pavé progression. | Consistent QC pass rate |
| **Master / lead** | Complex custom, mentoring apprentices, quality standard-setting. | Leadership |

Every stage is gated by [Quality Control](../03-operations/quality-control.md) standards — advancement is earned through demonstrated quality, not tenure.

## 8. Career ladders

Growth is visible and earned. Each department maintains a ladder tied to competencies and values, aligned with [Roles & Responsibilities](../02-governance/roles-and-responsibilities.md) and evaluated through [Performance Management](performance-management.md).

| Track | Illustrative progression |
|---|---|
| **Gemology** | Grading Assistant → Gemologist (GG) → Senior Gemologist → Head of Gemology |
| **Bench / craft** | Apprentice → Junior Setter → Setter → Master Setter / Bench Lead |
| **Sales / CX** | CX Associate → Consultant → Senior Consultant → CX Lead / Clienteling |
| **Operations** | Ops Associate → Specialist (inventory/fulfillment) → Ops Manager |
| **Compliance / finance** | Analyst → Specialist → Manager / Compliance Officer |

Both **individual-contributor** and **management** paths are valued — mastering the craft is a destination, not just a step toward managing.

## 10. Mentorship

Much of what makes someone excellent here is caught, not taught. Every new hire is paired with a **buddy** for their first 90 days, and craft roles have longer-term **mentors**.

- **Buddy (first 90 days)** — a peer who answers the "how do we actually do this here?" questions and helps someone feel at home.
- **Craft mentor** — for setters and gemologists, an experienced practitioner who guides skill progression and signs off on advancement (see §7).
- **Two-way benefit** — mentoring is a recognized leadership competency in [Performance Management](performance-management.md); teaching others is part of growing.

## 11. Measuring training effectiveness

Training only counts if it changes the work. We check that it does.

| Signal | What it tells us |
|---|---|
| **Completion & currency** | Required training done and up to date (compliance record). |
| **Assessment / demonstration** | Grading accuracy, bench sign-offs, mock-consultation quality. |
| **On-the-job outcomes** | First-pass QC rate, defect-escape rate, customer would-recommend rate. |
| **Credential attainment** | AJP / GG and other milestones earned. |
| **Feedback** | Learner and manager input on relevance and quality. |
