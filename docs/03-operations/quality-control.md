## 1. Purpose & scope

Quality Control (QC) is where *"Craftsmanship over shortcuts"* stops being a value statement and becomes a gate. This document defines the inspections, tolerances, and pass/fail criteria that every piece must clear before it is released to [Order Fulfillment](order-fulfillment.md) and shipped.

Scope covers:

- **Incoming stones** — diamonds and colored gemstones on receipt from suppliers.
- **In-process checks** — after setting, before finishing.
- **Final QC** — the last gate before a finished piece is released to ship.

It applies to catalog pieces, custom commissions, and repairs/resizes done in-house.

> **The rule that outranks the calendar:** we would rather delay a piece than ship one that isn't right. No piece ships on a QC "close enough." A fail is a fail.

## 3. Stage 1 — Incoming stone inspection

Performed on receipt, before a stone enters sellable inventory. This dovetails with receiving in [Sourcing & Procurement](sourcing-and-procurement.md) §7.4.

### 3.1 Matching the stone to its certificate — SOP

1. **Read the report number** from the GIA/IGI certificate.
2. **Verify online** via the lab's report-check service; confirm the record matches the paper/PDF report.
3. **Read the laser inscription** on the girdle under magnification; confirm it matches the report number.
4. **Weigh** the stone; confirm carat matches the report to 0.01 ct.
5. **Measure** dimensions (mm) and confirm against the report.
6. **Confirm shape and cut** match the report.
7. **Confirm color** against the stated grade (master set/loupe as needed).
8. **Confirm clarity** — locate the plotted inclusions/characteristics from the report's clarity plot under the microscope; confirm they match. A stone that doesn't match its plot is a red flag for a swapped stone.
9. **Confirm identity** (natural vs. lab-grown, or simulant screening) with tester as appropriate.
10. **Record** pass/fail with the inspector's initials and date against the inventory record.

### 3.2 Pass/fail — incoming stones

**PASS** requires all of: verified report, matching inscription, matching weight/measurements/shape, color and clarity consistent with the report, and confirmed identity.

**FAIL** on any of: missing/unverifiable report; inscription mismatch or absent where expected; weight/measurement outside tolerance (§7); clarity plot doesn't match; undisclosed treatment; identity mismatch. Failed stones are **quarantined** and a discrepancy is opened with the supplier (see [Vendor Management](vendor-management.md)).

## 4. Stage 2 — In-process (post-setting) inspection

After a stone is set and before finishing, inspect the setting itself. A perfect stone in an insecure setting is a failed piece.

### 4.1 Setting security & integrity checklist

- [ ] **Stone is secure** — no movement, rattle, or rotation when gently probed
- [ ] **Prong integrity** — all prongs present, evenly seated over the stone, no thin/undercut prongs, tips fully formed and closed
- [ ] **Bezel/channel** (where applicable) — metal is even and fully contacts the stone; no gaps
- [ ] **Pavé/micro-set** — each small stone secure, beads intact, no missing stones, even spacing
- [ ] **Seat depth** — girdle properly seated; culet not exposed to abrasion; table level
- [ ] **Symmetry & alignment** — stone centered and square to the mounting; multi-stone layouts aligned and level
- [ ] **No damage to the stone** — no chips, abrasions, or nicks introduced during setting (loupe/microscope the girdle and culet)
- [ ] **Gaps** — no visible gap between stone and metal that could catch or loosen

### 4.2 Pass/fail — setting

**FAIL** on any: movement in the stone; a short, thin, lifted, or damaged prong; a chip introduced in setting; a crooked or unlevel stone beyond tolerance; a missing melee stone. Fails return to the bench for correction and are re-inspected from the top of §4.1.

## 5. Stage 3 — Metal, hallmark & finish inspection

### 5.1 Metal & hallmark verification

- [ ] **Metal type & fineness** matches the order (e.g., 14k/18k gold, 950 platinum)
- [ ] **Hallmark / quality stamp** present, correct, and legible (e.g., `750`, `585`, `PT950`) `TODO: [Confirm Palencia's stamping/hallmarking standard and any maker's mark]`
- [ ] **Metal color** matches order (yellow / white / rose); rhodium plating on white gold even and complete where specified
- [ ] **Weight** within tolerance of spec (guards against porosity/undersupply)
- [ ] **No casting defects** — no porosity, pits, cracks, or inclusions in the metal

### 5.2 Finish inspection

- [ ] **Polish** — uniform, no scratches, drag lines, or dull spots under magnification
- [ ] **Surface** — no file marks, solder seams, or firescale
- [ ] **Edges & comfort** — no sharp edges; comfort-fit as specified; interior smooth
- [ ] **Texture/engraving** — matches design; engraving correct, centered, and clean (see engraving vendor QC in [Vendor Management](vendor-management.md))
- [ ] **Sizing** — ring size measured and matches order to tolerance
- [ ] **Consistency** — matched sets and bands are consistent with each other

## 6. Stage 4 — Final gemological verification & release

The last gate before the piece is released to ship.

### 6.1 Final QC — SOP

1. **Re-confirm stone identity to certificate.** Re-read the laser inscription on the *set* stone and confirm it still matches the certificate that will ship with the piece. This is the anti-swap control: the stone that leaves is the stone on the report.
2. **Re-verify the report** online one final time; confirm the certificate accompanying the order is the correct original.
3. **Confirm the piece matches the order and listing** — metal, stone(s), carat, grade, design.
4. **Full visual pass** at 10× — stone, setting, metal, finish, cleanliness.
5. **Clean** the piece; confirm no polishing compound, dust, or fingerprints remain.
6. **Weigh finished piece** and record.
7. **Photograph** for the order record. `TODO: [Confirm final-QC photo standard.]`
8. **Sign off.** Authorized final-QC inspector records PASS with initials, date, and the linked report number. Only a PASS releases the piece to [Order Fulfillment](order-fulfillment.md).

## 8. Defect logging & pass/fail governance

### 8.1 Logging

Every QC event is logged against the item/order with:

- Item/order ID and linked report number
- Stage (incoming / setting / metal-finish / final)
- Result (PASS / FAIL)
- Defect category and description (if fail)
- Inspector initials + date
- Disposition (corrected & re-inspected / quarantined / returned to supplier / scrapped)

### 8.2 Defect categories

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Stone–cert mismatch | Wrong weight, inscription mismatch, clarity plot mismatch, identity mismatch |
| Setting defect | Loose stone, short/thin/damaged prong, missing melee, crooked stone |
| Metal defect | Porosity, wrong fineness, missing/incorrect hallmark, wrong color |
| Finish defect | Scratches, file marks, firescale, incomplete rhodium |
| Spec mismatch | Wrong size, wrong design, wrong metal/stone vs. order |
| Documentation | Missing/incorrect certificate or disclosure |

### 8.3 Escalation & trends

- **Repeated defects from a supplier or vendor** feed the [Vendor Management](vendor-management.md) scorecard and can trigger corrective action or second-sourcing.
- **Recurring in-house defects** trigger a process/training review.

## 9. Master QC checklist (per finished piece)

- [ ] Incoming stone matched to certificate (report verified, inscription read)
- [ ] Stone identity confirmed (natural/lab/simulant screen)
- [ ] Stone set secure — no movement
- [ ] Prongs/bezel/channel integrity confirmed
- [ ] No damage introduced to stone during setting
- [ ] Symmetry & alignment within tolerance
- [ ] Metal type, fineness, and hallmark verified
- [ ] Metal color / plating correct and even
- [ ] Finish and polish clean under magnification
- [ ] Size within tolerance
- [ ] Final gemological re-verification (inscription re-read on set stone)
- [ ] Piece matches order and listing exactly
- [ ] Correct original certificate and disclosures assembled to ship
- [ ] Cleaned, photographed, weighed
- [ ] Final PASS signed off by authorized inspector
