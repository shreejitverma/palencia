## 1. Purpose & scope

Palencia Diamonds promises **free insured shipping with signature confirmation** on every order. This document defines how we honor that promise for a product where a single lost parcel can represent thousands of dollars and one of the biggest moments in a customer's life.

It covers outbound shipments, inbound returns and resizes, domestic and international transit, and the claims process. It applies to every physical shipment leaving or entering Palencia Diamonds.

Two non-negotiables govern everything below:

1. **Every shipment is insured for full value and requires a signature.** No exceptions, no "just this once."
2. **No shipment ever advertises its contents.** Packaging is discreet; nothing on the exterior says "jewelry" or shows our brand.

> This is an operational reference, **not legal advice.** Insurance terms, customs/export rules, and claims obligations must be confirmed with our insurer, carriers, and qualified counsel for each jurisdiction.

## 2. Carrier & service selection

Use a **jewelry-appropriate insured service** — a specialist jewelry carrier or a major carrier's high-value insured service — not standard retail shipping.

| High-value (≥ `[bracketed] TODO: threshold, e.g. $10,000`) | Specialist jewelry/secured carrier or carrier high-value insured service | **Adult signature (21+ where offered)** |
| International | Insured international express with export documentation | Adult signature |

**Selection rules**

- Match or exceed the piece's **full replacement value** with declared/insured value.
- Never use a service that permits delivery without a signature.
- Do not ship to **PO boxes** or **freight-forwarders** for insured high-value orders — carriers and insurers commonly exclude them.
- Prefer transit times that avoid a parcel sitting in a facility over a weekend.

## 3. Insurance & declared value

**Coverage principle:** Every parcel is insured door-to-door for **full replacement value** — the amount it would cost to remake/replace the piece, matching the enclosed appraisal.

| Field | Standard |
|---|---|
| Declared/insured value | Full replacement value of the piece(s) |
| Coverage type | `[bracketed] TODO: confirm — carrier declared-value vs. Jewelers Block / third-party transit insurance` |
| Who is insured in transit | Palencia Diamonds until delivery signature; then risk transfers to the customer on signed delivery |
| Proof retained | Insured value, tracking number, dispatch scan, and delivery signature on every order |

**Rules**

- Declared value must equal the appraisal value — never under-declare to reduce cost, and never over-declare.
- Insurance is void if signature requirements are waived — so they are never waived.
- Keep the carrier receipt/scan; it is the proof of insured tender if a claim is needed.

## 4. Discreet & secure packaging standards

**Goal:** A parcel that looks like anything but jewelry, and survives transit intact.

**Packaging checklist**

- [ ] Presentation box secured so the piece cannot move or rattle
- [ ] Inner box protected with cushioning; certificate, appraisal, and care card enclosed (see [Order Fulfillment](order-fulfillment.md))
- [ ] **Plain outer packaging** — no Palencia branding, no "jewelry," no logos, no gemstone imagery
- [ ] Return address is a plain/neutral label (no brand hints) `[bracketed] TODO: confirm return-address convention with insurer/carrier`
- [ ] Tamper-evident sealing
- [ ] Weight/dimensions recorded; label applied straight and scannable

> Discretion is a security control, not a style choice. A branded jewelry box on a doorstep is a target.

## 5. Signature requirements

Signature confirmation is part of the brand promise **and** a condition of insurance.

| Order type | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Standard | Signature confirmation on delivery |
| High-value / international | **Adult signature** (age-verified where the carrier offers it) |

- Signature can never be pre-waived by the customer or a delivery-instruction note. If the customer wants flexibility, route to a **secure alternative** (Section 7), not a no-signature delivery.
- The captured signature/delivery record is filed against the order as proof of delivery.

## 6. Domestic vs. international

### 6.1 Domestic

- Insured, signature-confirmed service to eligible addresses (no PO boxes/forwarders).
- Communicate the signature requirement and expected delivery window to the customer at dispatch.

### 6.2 International

International shipments add customs, duties, and legal complexity. Handle deliberately.

1. **Eligibility:** Confirm we ship to the destination country and that the items are not restricted for import/export there. `[bracketed] TODO: maintain an approved/restricted country list with Compliance.`
2. **Export documentation:** Complete the commercial invoice and any HS classification, certificate of origin, and Kimberley Process documentation required for diamonds.
3. **Duties & taxes:** Clearly tell the customer whether duties/import taxes are their responsibility (typically DDU/DAP) or prepaid (DDP). `[bracketed] TODO: confirm standard Incoterms and duty policy with Finance.`
4. **Sanctions & AML:** Screen the destination and recipient consistent with sanctions and FinCEN dealer rules before shipping (coordinate with Finance / Compliance).
5. **Insurance:** Confirm the insured service covers the full international transit and the destination country.
6. **Signature:** Adult signature on delivery.

## 7. Delivery to secure locations

When a customer cannot receive a signed delivery at home, offer a **secure** alternative — never a no-signature drop.

| Option | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Hold at carrier location / pickup point** | Customer collects with ID; signature captured |
| **Redirect to workplace** | Where a signature can be obtained |
| **Reschedule delivery** | Align to when someone can sign |
| **Carrier secure locker** | Where offered and insurable `[bracketed] TODO: confirm insurer acceptance` |

Coordinate redirects through the carrier's official channel and update the order record. Customer Experience proactively arranges this when a first attempt fails.

## 8. Lost, damaged, or stolen — claims process

Move fast; the insured tender and signature record are the backbone of any claim.

| Stage | Action | Owner | SLA |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Detect | Tracking stalls, delivery exception, or customer reports non-receipt/damage | CX | Same day |
| 2. Trace | Open a carrier trace/investigation | Operations | Within 1 business day |
| 3. Reassure customer | Tell the customer we're on it and that the piece is insured | CX | Within 1 business day |
| 4. Document | Gather tracking, insured value, dispatch scan, photos (damage), and signature/delivery record | Operations | 1–2 business days |
| 5. File claim | Submit to carrier/insurer with full documentation | Operations / Finance | Within `[bracketed] TODO: insurer filing window` |
| 6. Resolve for customer | Re-make/replace or refund per policy — do not make the customer wait on the insurer | CX / Finance | Begin remedy promptly |
| 7. Close | Recover from insurer; close claim; log root cause | Finance / Operations | On settlement |

**Principles**

- **The customer is made whole first.** We resolve generously and quickly and recover from the insurer separately — the customer's moment isn't held hostage to a claim timeline.
- Damage claims: retain all packaging and photograph everything before any handling.
- Theft/porch loss on a signature-required shipment is rare by design; if it occurs, the signature record drives the investigation.

## 9. Returns & resizing shipping (inbound)

We make returning and resizing safe and easy — see [Returns, Warranty & Resizing](../04-sales-cx/returns-warranty-resizing.md) for eligibility and windows.

1. Issue a **prepaid, insured, signature-required return label** for the full value of the piece.
2. Instruct the customer on **discreet packaging**: plain outer box, no branding, secure the piece so it can't move.
3. Require the customer to obtain a drop-off/tender receipt as proof of insured hand-off.
4. On arrival, inspect against the certificate and QC before refund, resize, or repair.
5. Resized/repaired pieces ship back to the customer under the same insured, signature-confirmed standard as a new order.

> Never ask a customer to ship a high-value piece back uninsured or without a signature. We supply the insured label and the instructions.
