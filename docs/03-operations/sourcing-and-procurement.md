## 1. Purpose & scope

This document is the standard operating procedure (SOP) for buying the raw materials that go into Palencia pieces:

- **Certified loose diamonds** (natural and, where labeled as such, lab-grown) with GIA or IGI reports.
- **Colored gemstones** (e.g., sapphire, emerald, ruby) used in custom and collection work.
- **Precious metals** — gold (yellow, white, rose) and platinum — as casting grain, sheet, wire, or finished mountings.
- **Findings and components** (settings, clasps, posts) where these are bought rather than made.

It does **not** cover the commercial relationship management side of suppliers (contracts, scorecards, offboarding) — see [Vendor Management](vendor-management.md) — nor the ethical framework itself, which lives in [Responsible Sourcing](../05-compliance-ethics/responsible-sourcing.md).

Our brand promise — *"Certified diamonds. Handcrafted jewelry. Honest prices."* — starts here. A stone we cannot certify, verify, and stand behind ethically never enters inventory.

## 2. Core principles

1. **Certification is non-negotiable.** Every diamond center stone enters inventory with an independent GIA or IGI report. No report, no purchase.
2. **Verify, don't trust.** We independently confirm every certificate and every stone against its certificate. A supplier's word is a starting point, never the finish line.
3. **Conflict-free or nothing.** Every rough-origin and polished-diamond purchase must be covered by the Kimberley Process and the System of Warranties. See [Responsible Sourcing](../05-compliance-ethics/responsible-sourcing.md).
4. **Honest prices require honest inputs.** We benchmark every purchase so our transparent pricing rests on real, defensible cost.
5. **Document everything.** Purchase orders, certificates, warranty statements, and receiving records are retained per our records schedule (see [Financial Controls](../08-finance/financial-controls.md)).

## 3. Supplier qualification

Before Palencia buys a single stone from a new supplier, that supplier must be qualified. Qualification is owned by Sourcing and signed off with Finance/Compliance.

### 3.1 Minimum qualification criteria

| Requirement | What we need on file |
|---|---|
| Legal identity | Registered business name, address, tax ID / VAT / EIN, ownership (KYC) |
| Trade standing | Membership in a recognized bourse or trade body (e.g., a WFDB-affiliated exchange), or verifiable trade references |
| Ethical warranties | Signed adherence to the **System of Warranties**; Kimberley Process compliance statement |
| RJC alignment | Responsible Jewellery Council (RJC) certification or a documented, credible path toward RJC Code of Practices alignment |
| Certification supply | Ability to supply GIA/IGI reports (or equivalent, pre-approved lab) with each stone |
| Sanctions/AML screening | Cleared against applicable sanctions lists; beneficial-ownership disclosed (see AML/KYC in [Financial Controls](../08-finance/financial-controls.md)) |
| References | Two trade references contacted and documented |

### 3.2 Qualification workflow

1. **Intake.** Sourcing collects the supplier's business documents, certifications, and warranty statements.
2. **Screening.** Compliance runs sanctions/AML and beneficial-ownership checks. `TODO: [Name the screening tool/service Palencia uses]`.

## 4. Certification requirements & authenticity verification

### 4.1 What we require

- **Diamonds:** an original GIA or IGI grading report tied to the exact stone. Preferred laboratories are **GIA** and **IGI**; any other lab requires prior written approval.
- **Colored gemstones:** a report from a recognized colored-stone laboratory identifying species, variety, and disclosing any treatments (e.g., heat, fracture-filling). `TODO: [Confirm Palencia's approved colored-stone labs, e.g., GIA, Gübelin, SSEF, AGL]`.
- **Treatment disclosure:** all treatments and, for diamonds, whether the stone is natural or laboratory-grown, must be disclosed in writing and reflected on the report and our listing.

### 4.2 Verifying certificate authenticity — SOP

A certificate is only useful if it is genuine and matches the stone in hand. For every diamond:

1. **Report-number lookup.** Enter the report number into the issuing lab's official online verification service:
   - GIA **Report Check** (verify report number ↔ 4Cs and details).
   - IGI **Report Verification** portal.
2. **Field-by-field match.** Confirm the online record matches the paper/PDF report and the physical stone: shape, measurements, carat weight, color, clarity, cut, and any comments.
3. **Laser inscription check.** Under magnification, read the laser inscription on the diamond's girdle (typically the report number). Confirm it matches the report and the online record. Record "inscription verified: yes/no".
4. **Tamper check.** Inspect the physical report for signs of alteration; confirm security features (hologram, microprint) where present.
5. **Escalate on any mismatch.** Any discrepancy between stone, report, and online record = **reject and quarantine** (see §7.4). Notify Compliance.

> A stone whose inscription or report cannot be verified is treated as unverified and cannot enter sellable inventory, regardless of supplier assurances.

### 4.3 Recording

Log the verified report number, lab, and inscription-check result against the stone's inventory record so the certificate travels with the stone through its whole life at Palencia. See traceability in [Inventory Management](inventory-management.md).

## 5. Buying criteria — the 4Cs and beyond

Palencia buys to a defined quality band so the collection is consistent and every listing is honest.

### 5.1 The 4Cs standard

| C | What we evaluate | Palencia buying guidance |
|---|---|---|
| **Cut** | Proportions, symmetry, polish, light performance | Prioritized first. For round brilliants, target **Excellent/Very Good** cut. `TODO: [Confirm minimum cut grade by shape]` |
| **Color** | GIA D–Z scale (colorless → light) | Standard collection band `TODO: [e.g., D–J]`; disclose exactly, never round up |
| **Clarity** | FL → I3, inclusions under 10× | Standard band `TODO: [e.g., FL–SI2]`; eye-clean threshold documented per listing |
| **Carat** | Weight to the hundredth | Buy to target weights that avoid "just under" waste; weight verified on receipt (§7) |

### 5.2 Beyond the 4Cs

- **Fluorescence:** disclose; avoid strong fluorescence that causes haziness in colorless stones.
- **Origin & type:** natural vs. lab-grown clearly labeled at every step. Country/known-origin captured where available for responsible-sourcing traceability.
- **Matching:** for pairs and layouts (studs, three-stone, tennis), confirm stones match in color, clarity, and cut within tolerance.
- **Make/quality of colored stones:** species/variety confirmed by report; treatments disclosed.

### 5.3 Rejection triggers

Reject any stone that: lacks a verifiable report; fails the inscription/authenticity check; does not match its certificate; falls outside our quality band without a documented exception; or carries undisclosed treatments.

## 6. Responsible & conflict-free sourcing requirements

Every purchase must satisfy our ethical requirements. This section is procedural; the policy lives in [Responsible Sourcing](../05-compliance-ethics/responsible-sourcing.md).

### 6.1 Required for every relevant purchase

- **Kimberley Process Certification Scheme (KPCS):** rough-diamond flows covered; conflict-free assurances documented.
- **System of Warranties (SoW):** every invoice for diamonds must carry the current World Diamond Council SoW statement, affirming conflict-free origin and compliance with resolutions and laws.
- **OECD Due Diligence Guidance:** risk-based due diligence on supply chains for minerals from conflict-affected and high-risk areas.
- **RJC alignment:** preference for RJC-certified suppliers; chain-of-custody documentation for metals where available.

### 6.2 SoW statement — what to check

On each diamond invoice, confirm the warranty language is present, current, and references the invoice. File it with the PO. A diamond invoice **without** a valid SoW statement is not payable until corrected.

### 7.2 Price benchmarking (before committing)

1. **Diamonds:** benchmark the quoted price against a recognized wholesale reference (e.g., a Rapaport-based percentage-off benchmark) for the exact shape/carat/color/clarity. `TODO: [Confirm Palencia's reference source and target discount band]`.
2. **Metals:** price against the live **spot** market (LBMA gold/platinum) plus an agreed fabrication premium.
3. **Colored stones:** benchmark against comparable recent buys and market comps; document reasoning where no clean index exists.
4. **Record** the benchmark and the variance from it on the PO. Purchases materially above benchmark require a written justification.

> Honest prices to customers depend on defensible input costs. Benchmarking is how we keep that promise structurally, not just aspirationally.

### 7.3 Raising the PO

A compliant purchase order includes:

- Approved supplier name + ASL reference.
- Line items: stone/metal description, quantity, carat/weight, and — for diamonds — the report number and lab.
- Agreed price, benchmark reference, and variance.
- Ethical terms: SoW required, KP compliance, RJC status noted.
- Delivery terms, insured-shipping requirement, and expected receipt date.
- Approver signature per authority limits.

### 7.4 Receiving & verification — SOP

On arrival, Receiving/QC performs an incoming inspection **before** goods are accepted into inventory or payment is released:

1. **Match to PO.** Confirm items, quantities, and descriptions against the PO.
2. **Certificate present.** Confirm each diamond arrives with its original GIA/IGI report and a valid SoW statement on the invoice.
3. **Authenticity check.** Run §4.2 (report-number lookup + inscription check) on every diamond.
4. **Physical verification.** Weigh each stone (carat to the hundredth) and confirm measurements match the report. `TODO: [Confirm calibrated scale/gauge used]`.
5. **Gemological spot-check.** Loupe/microscope confirmation that the stone matches its report (color, clarity characteristics/plot, cut). Detailed acceptance criteria live in [Quality Control](quality-control.md).
6. **Ethical documentation filed.** SoW statement and KP documentation attached to the receiving record.
7. **Decision.**
   - **Accept:** create/attach the inventory record, link the report number to the item, move to secure storage per [Inventory Management](inventory-management.md).
   - **Reject/quarantine:** any mismatch, missing report, failed authenticity check, or missing warranty → quarantine the goods, do not release payment, and open a supplier discrepancy in [Vendor Management](vendor-management.md).

### 7.5 Payment release

Finance releases payment only after: (a) receiving sign-off, (b) authenticity verified, (c) SoW/KP documentation on file, and (d) the invoice matches the PO (three-way match: PO ↔ goods ↔ invoice). See [Financial Controls](../08-finance/financial-controls.md).
