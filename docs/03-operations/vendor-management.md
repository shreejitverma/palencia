## 1. Purpose & scope

Palencia is only as trustworthy as its supply chain. This document governs the **commercial and relationship** side of that chain — who we work with, on what terms, and how we hold them accountable. The buying mechanics (POs, benchmarking, receiving) live in [Sourcing & Procurement](sourcing-and-procurement.md); the ethical framework lives in [Responsible Sourcing](../05-compliance-ethics/responsible-sourcing.md).

### Vendor categories in scope

| Category | Examples of what they provide |
|---|---|
| **Diamond & gemstone dealers** | Certified loose diamonds, colored stones, melee |
| **Metal / bullion suppliers** | Gold, platinum casting grain, sheet, wire |
| **Casting houses** | Casting mountings from wax/CAD |
| **Setting / bench partners** | Outsourced stone-setting (if any) |
| **Plating / rhodium** | White-gold rhodium plating, finishing |
| **Engraving** | Hand and machine engraving |
| **Certification / appraisal** | Lab reports, independent appraisals |
| **Shipping & insurance partners** | Insured carriers, jewelers block insurer |
| **Packaging / other** | Boxes, inserts, consumables |

## 2. Principles

1. **Due diligence before dollars.** No vendor is paid before they're qualified and onboarded.
2. **Ethics travel through the whole chain.** Every relevant vendor signs ethical-sourcing warranties — not just our diamond dealers.
3. **Measure, don't assume.** Vendors are scored on real performance data, reviewed on a cadence.
4. **Never single-threaded on the critical stuff.** Anything that can stop us shipping needs a backup source.
5. **Offboard cleanly.** Exits protect our goods, our data, and our customers.

### 3.2 Additional due diligence by category

| Category | Extra requirements |
|---|---|
| Diamond/gem dealers | GIA/IGI supply capability; **System of Warranties** adherence; Kimberley Process compliance; RJC certification or credible path; bourse/WFDB affiliation |
| Metal suppliers | Responsible-metals chain of custody where available (RJC Chain-of-Custody / recycled content); LBMA-referenced pricing |
| Casting / setting / plating / engraving | Quality samples pass [Quality Control](quality-control.md); handling of customer/owned stones under insured care, custody & control; secure logistics |
| Shipping & insurance | Licensed, insured, signature-confirmation capable; specialist jewelry coverage; claims track record |
| Appraisal / certification | Independence; recognized credentials (e.g., GIA-trained, accredited) |

> Ethical due diligence is not a diamond-only concern. A casting house or plater that mishandles labor or environmental standards is a reputational risk to the Palencia name just as much as a non-conflict-free stone.

## 4. Onboarding

### 4.1 Onboarding SOP

1. **Qualify** — complete §3 due diligence; document findings.
2. **Contract** — execute agreement with terms, SLAs, and warranties (§6).
3. **Ethical warranties** — collect signed System of Warranties / responsible-sourcing attestation (§7).
4. **Set up records** — add to the vendor master with category, owner, terms, insurance expiry, and contacts.
5. **Approve** — add to the **Approved Supplier List (ASL)** with scope and approval date; only ASL vendors may receive POs.
6. **Trial period** — first orders monitored closely; results captured on the scorecard (§5).
7. **Confirm backup** — record the second source for this category (§8).

### 4.2 Vendor master record — required fields

Legal name • category • primary contact • approval date & scope • contract & SLA reference • ethical warranty on file (Y/N) • insurance certificate & expiry • payment terms • scorecard link • backup/second source • status (Active / Probation / Suspended / Offboarded).

### 5.3 Rating bands & actions

| Overall score | Band | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 4.5 – 5.0 | Preferred | Grow share; consider preferred terms |
| 3.5 – 4.4 | Approved | Continue; monitor normally |
| 2.5 – 3.4 | Watch | Corrective action plan; increased monitoring; activate backup |
| < 2.5 | At risk | Probation → offboarding if not remedied |
| Any critical failure | — | Immediate suspension pending review (see §5.4) |

### 5.4 Critical failures (auto-escalate regardless of score)

- A **certificate/stone integrity** failure (fake report, swapped stone, undisclosed treatment or lab-grown).
- An **ethical breach** (missing/false SoW, Kimberley Process or sanctions issue).
- Loss or mishandling of goods in the vendor's care, custody & control.
- Repeated safety/security lapses in handling our high-value goods.

## 7. Ethical-sourcing warranties

- **Diamond/gem vendors:** current **World Diamond Council System of Warranties** statement on every invoice; Kimberley Process compliance; RJC certification or documented path.
- **Metal vendors:** responsible/recycled-sourcing attestation; chain-of-custody where available.
- **All vendors:** signed responsible-business attestation covering labor and environmental standards, aligned to the OECD Due Diligence Guidance and Palencia's [Responsible Sourcing](../05-compliance-ethics/responsible-sourcing.md) policy.
- **Maintenance:** warranties refreshed at least annually and on renewal; lapses move the vendor to **Watch** until cured.

## 8. Backup & second-source strategy

Continuity is a control, not a luxury — a supplier problem must never become a customer promise broken.

- **Single-source risk register:** every critical category lists its primary and at least one qualified **backup** vendor. `TODO: [Confirm which categories are single-sourced today and the plan to close gaps.]`
- **Qualified, not just identified:** backups are pre-qualified (§3) and ideally trialed, so they can be activated quickly.
- **Trigger to activate:** a Watch/At-risk rating, a critical failure, a capacity/lead-time breach, or geopolitical/supply disruption.
- **Balanced allocation:** avoid over-concentration with any one vendor for critical inputs.
- **Design for substitutability:** favor certified stones and standard specs that more than one qualified vendor can supply.

## 9. Offboarding

### 9.1 Offboarding SOP

1. **Decide & document** the reason (performance, ethics, strategy, vendor exit).
2. **Recover goods** — return/settle all consignment/memo goods and any Palencia/customer property in the vendor's custody; reconcile per [Inventory Management](inventory-management.md).
3. **Settle accounts** — close open POs and invoices; three-way match final payments.
4. **Revoke access** — remove system access, portals, and data sharing; confirm return/destruction of confidential and customer data.
5. **Update records** — set status to Offboarded on the vendor master; remove from the Approved Supplier List.
6. **Confirm continuity** — verify the backup/second source is active for the category.
7. **Retain records** — keep contracts, warranties, and history per the retention schedule.

### 9.2 Emergency suspension

A critical failure (§5.4) triggers **immediate suspension**: halt new POs, quarantine affected goods, notify Finance/Compliance, and open an investigation before any offboarding or reinstatement decision.
