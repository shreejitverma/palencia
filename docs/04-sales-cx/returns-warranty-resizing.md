# Part A — Customer policy

## 1. Our promise

Buying fine jewelry online is a big act of trust. We make it low-risk on purpose:

- **Free insured shipping**, both ways on eligible returns, with signature confirmation.
- **Complimentary resizing** where the design allows.
- **Manufacturing-defect coverage** on craftsmanship.
- **Lifetime care** — cleaning, inspection, and prong-tightening — for pieces bought from us.
- **A real human** to help at every step.

## 4. Manufacturing-defect warranty

We stand behind our craftsmanship. If a piece fails due to a **defect in materials or workmanship**, we repair or replace it.

### Covered

- [ ] Loose or lost stones due to a **setting defect** (not impact)
- [ ] **Prong or setting failure** under normal wear
- [ ] **Clasp or joint** manufacturing failures
- [ ] Metal or finish defects present from manufacture
- [ ] Coverage period: `TODO: [Confirm — e.g., lifetime manufacturing-defect coverage / limited period]`

### Not covered (exclusions)

- Normal wear (surface scratches, patina, gradual thinning of the band over years)
- **Accidental damage, impact, or loss** (this is what insurance is for — see below)
- Damage from **third-party repair/resizing/alteration**
- Loss or theft of the piece or a stone due to impact/accident
- Chemical damage, improper storage, or failure to follow care guidance
- Lost stones where the setting is intact and undamaged (indicates impact)

### Warranty ≠ insurance

We recommend every customer insure their piece against **loss, theft, and accidental damage** — our warranty covers manufacturing defects only. Every piece ships with the documentation needed to insure it. See [Shipping & Insurance](../03-operations/shipping-and-insurance.md).

## 8. SOP — Returns & exchanges

1. **Verify eligibility** against §2 (within window, condition, certificate/packaging present).
2. **Issue a prepaid, insured, signature-required return label.** Never let a customer self-insure a valuable piece.
3. On arrival, **route to QC** for inspection and authentication (confirm it's the same certified stone via report number and any internal ID). See [Quality Control](../03-operations/quality-control.md).
4. **QC confirms** condition and identity; flags any wear/alteration.
5. **Process refund/exchange** per §2. For exchanges, transparently charge/refund the difference.
6. **Close the loop** with the customer and confirm satisfaction.
7. **Log reason** for the return for trend analysis (fit, style, changed mind, quality).

> **Fraud/authentication note:** Stone-swap risk is real in this industry. Every returned piece is verified against its GIA/IGI report number and internal record before a refund is issued. `TODO: [Confirm authentication procedure and who performs it]`.

## 9. SOP — Resizing

1. **Check the design** against §3 to confirm resizing is feasible; if not, explain honestly and offer alternatives.
2. **Confirm target size** (ideally via a printed sizer or professional measurement).
3. **Issue insured, prepaid shipping** both ways.
4. **Production resizes**, then the piece goes through **QC** (stone security, finish) before return.
5. **Confirm complimentary vs. chargeable** per §3 and communicate clearly *before* work if any charge applies.
6. **Update the customer** with timeline and tracking.

## 10. SOP — Manufacturing-defect claims

1. **Gather evidence** — photos, description, delivery date, wear history.
2. **Make an initial defect-vs-damage assessment** (see §4). When ambiguous, **route to QC/Production for a definitive call** — do not deny on the front line.
3. **If defect:** repair or replace at no cost; expedite insured shipping at our cost.
4. **If accidental damage/loss:** explain kindly that this falls under insurance, not warranty; offer paid repair and support the insurance claim with documentation.
5. **Default to generosity** on genuine ambiguity — a repaired customer relationship is worth more than the repair. Escalate borderline cost decisions per authority limits in [Customer Experience Standards](customer-experience-standards.md).
6. **Log root cause.** Recurring defect types feed back to Production/QC.

## 11. SOP — Lifetime care

1. Confirm the piece was purchased from Palencia (order/certificate lookup).
2. Advise the appropriate service (§5) and any cost up front.
3. Insured, prepaid shipping both ways.
4. QC performs inspection; flag and quote any needed repair *before* doing it.
5. Return with a short "here's what we did / condition report" note.

## 12. SOP — Arrived damaged or wrong item

1. **Apologize and own it immediately** — this is a service-recovery moment (LEARN framework, see [Customer Experience Standards](customer-experience-standards.md)).
2. **Reship the correct/undamaged item** on priority, insured, at our cost.
3. **Recover the incorrect item** with a prepaid insured label.
4. **File the shipping insurance claim** if the piece was damaged in transit; see [Shipping & Insurance](../03-operations/shipping-and-insurance.md).
5. **Root-cause** with Operations/QC to prevent recurrence.
