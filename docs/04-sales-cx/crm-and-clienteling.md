## 1. Why clienteling matters here

Our vision is to be the company customers **recommend without hesitation** and **buy from again**. A fine-jewelry customer isn't a one-time transaction — an engagement ring buyer becomes a wedding-band buyer, an anniversary-gift buyer, a "push present" buyer, and a referral source for their whole friend group.

Clienteling is how we remember, anticipate, and serve those moments — **tastefully, never intrusively.** The line we hold: everything we do should feel *thoughtful*, not *targeted*. If a follow-up would make a customer feel surveilled or sold-to, we don't send it.

## 2. Capturing customer data — with consent

We only collect what helps us serve the customer better, and we're transparent about it. Data practices follow [Data Privacy & Security](../05-compliance-ethics/data-privacy-and-security.md) and applicable law (e.g., `TODO: [confirm GDPR/CCPA applicability by market]`).

### What we capture

| Data | Why | Consent basis |
|---|---|---|
| Name, contact info | Service the order & reach them | Necessary for purchase |
| Purchase history & certificates | Warranty, care, recommendations | Necessary for purchase |
| Important dates (anniversary, birthday, wedding) | Timely, helpful gift reminders | **Opt-in** |
| Style/size preferences, partner details | Personalize recommendations | Provided by customer |
| Marketing preferences & channels | Send only what they want | **Opt-in**, per-channel |

### Consent principles

- **Marketing communications are opt-in**, with an easy opt-out in every message.
- **Separate transactional from marketing** — order updates always send; promotions only with consent.
- **Minimize:** collect only what earns its keep. No "just in case" data hoarding.
- **Be honest about use.** Never surprise a customer with how we used their data.
- **Honor deletion/access requests** promptly per [Data Privacy & Security](../05-compliance-ethics/data-privacy-and-security.md).

## 6. Important-date reminders (repeat gifting)

Our single highest-value, most-appreciated clienteling tool — done right.

### How it works

1. Customer **opts in** to date reminders (anniversary, birthday, partner's dates).
2. We set a reminder `TODO: [confirm lead time — e.g., 3–4 weeks ahead]` so there's time to choose and ship.
3. The advisor sends a **warm, helpful, no-pressure** note — a service, not a sales blast.

### Script — anniversary reminder

> "Hi [Name] — your `[Nth]` anniversary is coming up on `[date]`, and I remembered you might want to mark it. No pressure at all, but if you'd like a hand finding something she'd love — or just ideas — I'm here. Either way, congratulations in advance on the milestone."

**Guardrails:** opt-in only; easy to turn off; never guilt-based ("don't forget!"); frequency-capped so we're a helpful memory, not a nag.

## 7. Follow-up cadences

Structured, but always human and always skippable if the customer signals they've had enough. Cadence complements the post-purchase check-ins in [Customer Experience Standards](customer-experience-standards.md).

### Post-purchase (new customer)

| Timing | Touch | Intent |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery + 2 days | "Did it arrive perfectly? Fit okay?" | Care / catch issues |
| Delivery + 2 weeks | Care tips + how to reach us | Reassurance |
| ~30 days | "How did the moment go?" + review invite *if delighted* | Connection |
| ~6 months | Complimentary cleaning/inspection reminder | Service |
| Annually | Care reminder + important-date check-in | Lifetime relationship |

### Prospect (didn't buy)

| Timing | Touch | Intent |
|---|---|---|
| Same day | Certificates + shortlist recap | Helpful |
| Day 2–3 | Gentle "any questions?" (only if engaged) | Support |
| Day 7 | One value-add (education), then stop | Respectful |

**Rule:** two respectful, unanswered touches is the ceiling before we let it rest. Silence is a valid answer and we honor it.

## 8. Win-back (dormant customers)

For customers who've gone quiet but haven't opted out:

- **Lead with value or care**, not a discount plea (e.g., "your ring is due for a free inspection").
- **Reference the relationship**, not just the transaction.
- **One respectful attempt**, maybe two spaced out — then stop.
- **Honor opt-outs instantly.**

### Script — win-back

> "Hi [Name] — it's been a little while, and I was thinking of you. Your piece from us is due for its complimentary cleaning and inspection whenever it's convenient — a good way to keep it secure and brilliant. And if a new occasion is ever on the horizon, I'd be glad to help. No pressure at all."

## 9. Reviews & referrals — done tastefully

Reviews and referrals are the **result** of genuine service, not a campaign we run at people. Our value: we treat every review, good or bad, as a signal to improve.

### Principles

- **Ask only the clearly delighted**, and only after the moment has landed well.
- **Make it easy**, never obligatory. One ask, no repeated nagging.
- **Never incentivize dishonest reviews.** If we offer any referral perk, it must be transparent and disclosed. `TODO: [Confirm referral program terms, if any, and disclosure requirements]`
- **Respond to every review** — thank the happy ones, and address the critical ones with humility and a fix.
- **Route negative feedback into service recovery** (LEARN framework, [Customer Experience Standards](customer-experience-standards.md)) *before* it becomes a public review where possible.

### Script — inviting a review

> "It's been wonderful helping with such a special piece, and I'm so glad the moment went beautifully. If you ever feel like sharing your experience in a review, it genuinely helps other couples shopping with the same nerves you had. Only if you're inclined — either way, thank you for trusting us."

### Script — referral (natural, not transactional)

> "If a friend of yours ever finds themselves ring-shopping and feeling overwhelmed, I'd be honored to help them the way I helped you. Feel free to send them my way."

## 10. Data hygiene & governance

- **Keep records accurate.** Update after every meaningful interaction.
- **De-duplicate** customer records so no one gets doubled-up messaging.
- **Respect frequency caps** across all cadences combined — count total touches, not per-campaign.
- **Audit consent regularly**; purge or anonymize per the retention schedule and [Data Privacy & Security](../05-compliance-ethics/data-privacy-and-security.md).
- **Segment lists feed marketing** in line with [Marketing Strategy](../06-marketing/marketing-strategy.md), always consent-first.

## 11. The clienteling standard, restated

Remember what matters, reach out with genuine care, and always give the customer an easy way to say "not now." Thoughtful beats targeted. A customer who feels *known and cared for* — not *tracked and marketed to* — is the customer who comes back and brings their friends.
