# Customer Experience Standards

> The Palencia service standard — how fast we respond, how we speak, how we keep customers calm and informed through a big purchase, and how we recover when something goes wrong.

## 1. The standard, in one line

**Treat every customer as if they were our only one** — because to them, this purchase is one of the most important they'll ever make. Our vision is measured by trust: the share of customers who'd buy again and refer us. This document is how we earn that, interaction by interaction.

Everyone who touches a customer — sales, support, production updates, shipping — is held to these standards.

## 2. Response-time SLAs by channel

Speed is a form of respect. These are our **maximum** response times during business hours; faster is always better. A response can be an answer *or* an honest "I'm on it, here's when I'll have your answer."

| Channel | First response (business hours) | First response (after hours) | Full resolution target |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Live chat** | Under 2 minutes | Auto-acknowledge + next-business-hour reply | Same session where possible |
| **Phone** | Answer within 4 rings; voicemail returned same business day | Voicemail returned next business day | Same/next day |
| **Email** | Within 4 business hours | Within next business day | 1–2 business days |
| **Video appointment** | Confirmed within 4 business hours; honored on time | — | Per appointment |
| **Social / DM** | Within 4 business hours | Next business day | 1–2 business days |
| **Custom-design inquiry** | Within 1 business day (acknowledgement + next steps) | — | Per project plan |
| **Post-purchase / warranty claim** | Acknowledge within 1 business day | — | See [Returns, Warranty & Resizing](returns-warranty-resizing.md) |

### SLA principles

- **Acknowledge fast, even without the full answer.** "Got your message — I'm checking on this and will have an answer by 3pm today" beats silence.
- **No customer waits without knowing when they'll hear back.**
- **Set a next step every time.** Every interaction ends with who does what, by when.

## 3. Tone & etiquette

We sell emotional, high-stakes purchases. Our voice mirrors the brand: **clear, warm, honest, precise.**

### Do

- **Lead with warmth.** Congratulate the moment. This is joyful for them.
- **Be plain-spoken.** No jargon without explaining it; no corporate filler.
- **Be honest, always** — about grades, prices, timelines, and mistakes.
- **Be precise.** Correct carat, grade, report number, ship date. In a certified-diamond business, a small error breaks trust.
- **Mirror the customer's pace and formality.**
- **Own it in the first person.** "I'll take care of this," not "the system won't let me."

### Don't

- No high-pressure tactics, false urgency, or manufactured scarcity — this violates our values.
- No overselling a grade or glossing over a trade-off.
- No jargon-dumping to sound authoritative.
- No leaving a customer without a clear next step.
- No blaming the customer, a supplier, or "the policy."

### Etiquette quick-reference

| Situation | Say | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Greeting | "Congratulations — happy to help you get this right." | "How can I help you today?" (generic) |
| Don't know an answer | "Great question — let me confirm and get back to you by [time]." | Guessing |
| Delay | "I want to be upfront: this will take until [date]. Here's why." | Hiding it and hoping |
| Customer is anxious | "You're asking exactly the right questions. Let's go through it." | Rushing them |
| Mistake on our side | "That's on us. Here's how I'll fix it." | Excuses |

## 4. Personalization

Generic service is forgettable; personal service is referable.

- **Use the customer's name** and reference *their* specific moment and preferences.
- **Remember context** across conversations — never make them repeat themselves. Log it in the [CRM](crm-and-clienteling.md).
- **Tailor recommendations** to what they told us (style, budget, lifestyle), not to what we want to move.
- **Match the channel** to the customer — some want a video appointment, some want a quick text-based chat.
- **Capture the details that matter**: partner's style, the story behind the piece, important dates.

## 5. Proactive updates during production & shipping

Silence is where anxiety grows. For a purchase this significant, **we update customers before they have to ask.**

### Standard proactive touchpoints

| Milestone | We proactively send | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| Order confirmed | Order summary + what happens next + timeline | Email |
| Certified stone allocated / QC started | "Your piece is in production" note | Email |
| Custom design milestones | Design proof, approval requests, progress | Email / video |
| Quality control passed | "It passed inspection and is being prepared to ship" | Email |
| Shipped | Tracking + insured/signature-required note + delivery window | Email/SMS |
| Out for delivery | Reminder that signature is required | SMS/email |
| Delivered | Confirmation + care welcome + how to reach us | Email |

### If a timeline slips

Tell the customer **as soon as we know**, not when the date passes. Honesty over optimism.

> "I want to keep you fully in the loop: your piece needs an extra [X days] because [honest reason — e.g., our QC team flagged a setting detail we want perfect before it ships]. Here's the new date: [date]. I'm sorry for the change, and I'd rather it be right than fast. Anything I can do to help on the timing?"

## 6. Handling anxiety around a big purchase

Most customers are spending more than they usually would, on something irreversible, for someone they love. Nervousness is normal and healthy.

**How we lower the temperature:**

- **Normalize it.** "It's a big decision — that's exactly why we take our time."
- **Educate, don't pressure.** Confidence comes from understanding (see [Sales Playbook](sales-playbook.md)).
- **Make risk small.** Restate the guarantees: free insured shipping, complimentary resizing where the design allows, manufacturing-defect coverage, returns.
- **Give permission to slow down.** "There's no rush. A ring is forever; the decision can take a week."
- **Be reachable.** A named human they can come back to beats a faceless queue.

## 7. Post-purchase care & check-ins

The relationship starts, not ends, at delivery. See [CRM & Clienteling](crm-and-clienteling.md) for full cadences.

| When | Touch | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery + 2 days | "Did it arrive perfectly? How's the fit?" | Catch sizing/issues early |
| Delivery + 2 weeks | Care tips + resizing reminder | Reassurance, service |
| ~30 days | "How did the moment go?" (if a proposal) | Human connection, review invite if delighted |
| Annually | Complimentary care reminder (cleaning/inspection) | Lifetime relationship |
| Important dates | Anniversary/birthday reminders for repeat gifting | Thoughtful, not salesy |

Post-purchase check-ins are about **care first**. Reviews and referrals are a byproduct of genuine service, invited tastefully and only when the customer is clearly happy.

## 8. Service-recovery playbook

When something goes wrong — a delay, a defect, a sizing miss, a shipping problem — recovery is our chance to *deepen* trust. Our value: *we resolve complaints generously and quickly.*

### The LEARN framework

| Step | What we do |
|---|---|
| **L — Listen** | Let the customer explain fully. Don't interrupt or defend. |
| **E — Empathize** | Acknowledge the impact, sincerely. "I completely understand why this is upsetting." |
| **A — Apologize & own** | Take responsibility without excuses, even if the cause is external. |
| **R — Resolve** | Fix it, fast. Offer the remedy and, where warranted, a goodwill gesture. |
| **N — Note & next-step** | Log it, close the loop, confirm the customer is satisfied, and fix the root cause. |

### Script — service recovery

> "Thank you for telling me, and I'm genuinely sorry — this isn't the experience we promise. Here's what I'm going to do: [specific fix] by [date]. I'll personally follow this through and update you at every step. Is that the outcome you're hoping for, or is there something else that would make this right?"

### Remedy ladder

1. **Fix the problem** (repair, resize, reship, refund per policy).
2. **Cover the friction** (expedited insured shipping at our cost, waived fees).

## 10. The standard, restated

Fast, honest, warm, precise, and present — before, during, and long after the sale. If an action wouldn't feel right done for a close friend buying their engagement ring, it doesn't meet the Palencia standard.
