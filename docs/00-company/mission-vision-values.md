## Mission — why we exist

**To make genuinely fine, independently certified diamond jewelry accessible through honest craftsmanship and honest prices — so that every meaningful moment can be marked without compromise or confusion.**

## Vision — where we're going

**To be the most trusted name in fine diamond jewelry: the company customers recommend without hesitation, and the standard the industry measures itself against.**

We measure "best in the industry" not by size, but by trust — the percentage of customers who would buy from us again and send us their family and friends.

## Values — how we work

Each value comes with the behavior that proves it. Values that don't change decisions aren't values.

### 1. Honesty is the product
We tell customers the truth — about a stone's grade, a price, a trade-off, a timeline. If we wouldn't say it to a close friend buying their engagement ring, we don't say it to a customer.
> **In practice:** Every center stone ships with an independent GIA/IGI report. Pricing is transparent and defensible. We never oversell a grade or hide a flaw.

### 2. Craftsmanship over shortcuts
Hand-set, inspected, and finished to a standard we'd be proud to wear ourselves. We would rather delay a piece than ship one that isn't right.
> **In practice:** Every piece passes [Quality Control](../03-operations/quality-control.md) before it leaves. No exceptions for deadlines.

### 3. The customer's moment, not our transaction
We are a small part of one of the biggest moments in someone's life. We act like it — with patience, care, and zero pressure.
> **In practice:** No high-pressure sales. Generous return, resizing, and warranty terms. We support the piece — and the customer — for life.

### 4. Do right by the earth and its people
We will only sell what we can stand behind ethically. Responsible, conflict-free sourcing is not a marketing line — it's a requirement.
> **In practice:** [Responsible Sourcing](../05-compliance-ethics/responsible-sourcing.md) — Kimberley Process, supplier due diligence, and warranties on every stone.

### 5. Earn trust, then earn it again
Reputation is built one honest interaction at a time and lost in one dishonest one. Every employee is a steward of the Palencia name.
> **In practice:** We resolve complaints generously and quickly, and we treat every review — good or bad — as a signal to improve.

### 6. Precision in everything
In a certified-diamond business, details are not details — they are the whole thing. A wrong carat weight, a misquoted grade, a late ship date all break trust.
> **In practice:** We check twice, document everything, and follow the [SOPs](../03-operations/). Accuracy is a discipline, not a talent.

## Brand promise

> **Certified diamonds. Handcrafted jewelry. Honest prices.**

This is the shorthand for everything above — the promise we make to customers and the bar we hold ourselves to internally.
