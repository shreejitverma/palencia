## 1. Who we are

Palencia Diamonds is an online-first fine jewelry company built on a simple, uncompromising idea: **certified diamonds, handcrafted jewelry, and honest prices — for everyone, not just the few.**

We sell the pieces that mark life's most meaningful moments — engagements, weddings, anniversaries, milestones — and we back every one with independent certification, transparent pricing, and service that treats each customer as if they were our only one.

## 2. What we sell

| Category | Includes |
|---|---|
| **Engagement rings** | Solitaire, halo, three-stone, hidden halo, custom |
| **Wedding bands** | Classic, pavé, eternity, matching sets |
| **Necklaces** | Chains, station, tennis, rivière |
| **Earrings** | Studs, hoops, drops, dangles |
| **Bracelets** | Tennis, bangles, station |
| **Pendants & charms** | Solitaire, halo, cluster, initials |
| **Custom design** | Made-to-order rings with customer choice of material, stone, and carat |

Every center stone comes with an independent grading report from **GIA** or **IGI**. Pieces are hand-set in **gold** (yellow, white, rose) and **platinum**.

## 3. How we're different

1. **Certification, always.** Independent GIA/IGI reports on center stones — not our own word for it.
2. **Handcrafted quality.** Hand-set pieces, not mass-stamped goods.
3. **Honest prices.** Transparent, defensible pricing without the traditional retail markup theater.
4. **Confidence built in.** Free insured shipping with signature confirmation, complimentary resizing where the design allows, and manufacturing-defect coverage.
5. **Made yours.** Custom design lets customers choose material, stone, and carat.

See [Brand Positioning](../01-brand/brand-positioning.md) for the full competitive framing.

## 4. Business model

- **Channel:** Primarily direct-to-consumer e-commerce via www.palenciadiamonds.com.
- **Fulfillment:** Certified stones sourced from vetted suppliers; pieces hand-set and quality-controlled before insured shipment to the customer.
- **Revenue:** Product sales (catalog + custom commissions). See [Pricing Strategy](../08-finance/pricing-strategy.md).

## 5. How the company is organized

Palencia Diamonds is an established company (10+ team members) organized into functional departments. See the [Organizational Structure](../02-governance/organizational-structure.md) and [Roles & Responsibilities](../02-governance/roles-and-responsibilities.md) for the full map.

At a glance, the core functions are:

- **Merchandising & Sourcing** — buying certified stones and designing the collection
- **Production & Quality Control** — hand-setting and inspecting every piece
- **Sales & Customer Experience** — guiding customers and supporting them for life
- **Marketing & Brand** — growing awareness and demand
- **Operations & Fulfillment** — inventory, shipping, insurance, logistics
- **Finance & Compliance** — pricing, controls, AML/KYC, responsible sourcing
- **People & Culture** — hiring, developing, and caring for the team
