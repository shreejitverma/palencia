## 1. Purpose of this plan

This document translates our [mission and vision](mission-vision-values.md) into a concrete, measurable three-year path. It exists so that everyone at Palencia Diamonds — from sourcing to fulfillment to customer experience — can see where we are going, why, and how their work moves us there.

Our vision is unambiguous: **to be the most trusted name in fine diamond jewelry.** This plan defines what "most trusted" means in practice, sets the metrics that prove it, and sequences the work to get there.

## 2. Definition of best-in-industry (trust-based, not size-based)

The diamond industry has historically measured success by scale — store count, ad spend, carats moved, market share. We reject that yardstick. A company can be enormous and still be distrusted; in fine jewelry, many are.

We define **best-in-industry** as the company that earns the highest trust per customer. Concretely, we are winning when:

| Trust signal | What it proves |
|---|---|
| Customers buy from us again | The purchase held up — the stone, the price, the service |
| Customers refer family and friends | They stake their own reputation on ours |
| Reviews are specific, unprompted, and glowing | We exceeded expectations on an emotional purchase |
| Independent certification matches what we said, every time | Our word and the lab report never diverge |
| Complaints are rare and, when they happen, resolved generously | We treat trust as recoverable only through honesty |

> We would rather be the smallest company that no customer regrets buying from than the largest company that many do. Trust compounds; scale without trust decays.

## 3. Strategic pillars

Every initiative in this plan ladders up to one of six pillars. They are not equal-weighted departments — they are the load-bearing walls of the brand.

### Pillar 1 — Trust & certification
Independent GIA/IGI grading on every center stone, presented plainly, never spun. The certificate is not a marketing prop; it is the proof that our word is good. We make certification easy to read, easy to verify, and impossible to misread.

### Pillar 2 — Craftsmanship
Hand-set, inspected, finished to a standard we'd wear ourselves. Craftsmanship is what makes a certified stone into a piece worth keeping for generations. We would rather delay a piece than ship one that isn't right.

### Pillar 3 — Honest pricing
Transparent, defensible prices without the traditional retail markup theater. Our pricing should survive a customer comparing us line-by-line against any competitor — and win on honesty, not just number.

### Pillar 4 — Customer experience
Zero-pressure guidance, generous service terms, and support for the life of the piece. We are a small part of one of the biggest moments in a customer's life, and we behave accordingly.

### Pillar 5 — Responsible sourcing
Conflict-free, ethically sourced stones and metals, backed by real due diligence — Kimberley Process, supplier warranties, and traceability where the supply chain allows. This is a requirement, not a campaign.

### Pillar 6 — Brand growth
Growth earned through reputation, not bought through hype. We grow by turning every satisfied customer into an advocate, and by making our standards visible to people who have been burned by the industry before.

## 5. Phased roadmap

### Year 1 (2026–2027) — Prove the promise, instrument the trust
**Theme: measure what we claim.**

- Stand up the full KPI dashboard above; establish baselines for every metric marked `TODO`.
- Formalize the certification presentation: every product page and shipment links the customer to their verifiable GIA/IGI report.
- Codify QC into documented SOPs with a first-inspection pass-rate target. See [Quality Control](../03-operations/quality-control.md).
- Publish transparent pricing logic internally so every customer-facing team member can explain any price. See [Pricing Strategy](../08-finance/pricing-strategy.md).
- Launch a structured post-purchase review-and-referral flow.
- Complete a responsible-sourcing due-diligence baseline audit of all active suppliers. See [Responsible Sourcing](../05-compliance-ethics/responsible-sourcing.md).

### Year 2 (2027–2028) — Deepen the moat, widen the audience
**Theme: turn proof into reputation.**

- Reach top-quartile service metrics (on-time shipping, first-response time, CSAT).
- Expand custom-design capability and lead times to make "made yours" a signature, not an exception.
- Build the referral program into a measurable revenue channel (target 40%+ organic/referral revenue share).
- Launch educational content that teaches customers to read a certificate and evaluate the 4Cs honestly — trust-building, not conversion-hacking.
- Introduce lifetime-service touchpoints (cleaning, inspection reminders, resizing) that keep us present after the sale.
- Achieve 100% documented chain-of-custody / supplier warranty coverage on eligible supply.

### Year 3 (2028–2029) — Set the standard
**Theme: become the yardstick.**

- Hit the Year 3 trust KPIs: NPS ≥ 75, referral rate 30%, LTV:CAC ≥ 5:1, review average ≥ 4.8.
- Reach near-perfect operational precision: order accuracy ≥ 99.9%, on-time shipping ≥ 99.5%.
- Publish an annual transparency report (sourcing, pricing philosophy, service performance) — putting our standards on record where the industry rarely does.
- Establish Palencia as a reference brand for trustworthy online diamond buying, cited by customers and reviewers as the honest option.

## 7. Risks & mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| A certification error or grade mismatch reaches a customer | Low | Severe (trust is binary here) | 100% independent-report verification; certification-match audits; immediate, generous remediation policy |
| Supply-chain disruption or a sourcing-integrity failure | Medium | High | Multi-supplier vetting; documented chain-of-custody; Kimberley Process & warranty requirements on every stone |
| Margin pressure erodes ability to keep service promises | Medium | High | Defensible pricing bands; disciplined CAC; grow referral share to lower acquisition cost |
| Growth outpaces our craftsmanship & QC capacity | Medium | High | Scale production and QC ahead of demand; never waive QC for deadlines |
| Reputational damage from a mishandled complaint | Medium | High | Fast, generous resolution SOPs; treat every review as a signal; empower CX to make it right |
| Regulatory exposure (AML/KYC for dealers in precious stones) | Medium | Medium | Compliance program aligned to FinCEN rules for dealers in precious metals/stones/jewels; counsel review |
| Commoditization / price-war pressure from big-box & online rivals | High | Medium | Compete on trust and craftsmanship, not price alone; make our standards visible |

> This plan is a strategic reference, **not legal advice**. Initiatives touching AML/KYC, privacy, and sourcing law must be reviewed by qualified counsel for each jurisdiction we operate in.

## 8. Governance & review

- **Quarterly:** Leadership reviews the KPI dashboard against targets and adjusts initiatives.
- **Annually:** This plan is re-reviewed, re-baselined, and rolled forward one year.
- **Accountability:** Each KPI has a named owner (see §6). A metric without an owner is not a real target.
