## 1. What this document is

This is the master reference for the Palencia brand identity. It defines the brand's promise, personality, pillars, and standards, and it points to the two systems that make the brand tangible: [Voice & Tone](voice-and-tone.md) (how we sound) and [Visual Identity](visual-identity.md) (how we look). Anyone creating something a customer will see — a product page, an email, a certificate insert, a package — should start here.

## 2. Brand promise

> **Certified diamonds. Handcrafted jewelry. Honest prices.**

This is the whole brand in six words. It is a promise, not a slogan — every part is a commitment we can prove:

| Promise | The proof behind it |
|---|---|
| **Certified diamonds** | Independent GIA/IGI report on every center stone |
| **Handcrafted jewelry** | Hand-set, individually QC-inspected pieces |
| **Honest prices** | Transparent, defensible, verifiable pricing |

If we can't prove a claim, we don't make it. That is the first rule of this brand.

## 3. Brand personality & archetype

**Primary archetype: the Trusted Expert / Guide.** We are the knowledgeable friend who happens to be a diamond expert — the one who tells you the truth even when it costs a sale, so you buy with confidence and come back for life.

**Secondary note: a touch of the Everyman.** We make fine jewelry accessible and unintimidating. We are not the aloof luxury house; we are expertise without condescension.

Our personality in five traits:

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Warm | Cold or formal |
| Honest | Salesy or spin-driven |
| Precise | Vague or hand-wavy |
| Confident | Arrogant or pushy |
| Generous | Transactional |

## 4. Brand pillars

Four pillars carry the brand. Every touchpoint should express at least one clearly.

1. **Certified** — Independent proof, always. Trust is our category.
2. **Handcrafted** — Made and inspected by people who care.
3. **Honest** — Transparent pricing and truthful grading.
4. **Yours** — Custom choice and lifetime service.

## 5. Tagline & promise usage

- **Primary promise/tagline:** *"Certified diamonds. Handcrafted jewelry. Honest prices."*
- Use the full three-part form in brand-level placements (homepage hero, packaging, brand campaigns).
- Keep the three clauses in order, each capitalized, each ending in a period. The rhythm is intentional.
- **Do not** alter the wording ("Certified gems," "Fair prices," etc.). It is fixed brand language.
- Short-form contexts (favicon alt text, tight UI) may use a single pillar word, but never a reworded tagline.

## 7. How the brand shows up across touchpoints

The brand must feel like one company everywhere. Each touchpoint has a job.

| Touchpoint | Brand job | Standards |
|---|---|---|
| **Website** | Build trust and make evaluation easy | Certification visible and verifiable on every stone; transparent pricing; no dark patterns or fake-scarcity timers; clear service promises |
| **Product pages** | Let the customer evaluate honestly | Real photography (see [Visual Identity](visual-identity.md)); complete, accurate 4Cs; link to the actual GIA/IGI report |
| **Packaging** | Make delivery feel like the moment it is | Considered, protective, gift-ready; full tagline; nothing disposable-feeling; insured & signature-confirmed |
| **Certificates & inserts** | Prove the promise in the customer's hands | Independent lab report included; plain-language explanation of what it means; care and service information |
| **Email** | Guide, reassure, and stay present for life | Helpful over promotional; order/shipping transparency; post-purchase care and service touchpoints (see Voice & Tone) |
| **Social** | Show craftsmanship and honesty in public | Real pieces, real education, real customers (with consent); no hype, no pressure |
| **Customer service** | Turn problems into trust | Fast, warm, generous resolution; empowered to make it right |

## 8. Brand do's & don'ts

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Show the certificate and let it speak | Ask customers to "trust us" on a grade |
| State prices plainly and explain them | Use markup-then-discount theater or fake countdowns |
| Describe stones with exact, truthful specs | Oversell a grade or hide an inclusion |
| Guide without pressure | Push, upsell, or manufacture urgency |
| Use real photography of real pieces | Use misleading or over-retouched stone imagery |
| Talk about sourcing honestly and specifically | Make vague "ethical" claims we can't document |
| Keep the tagline exact | Reword or paraphrase the brand promise |
| Resolve complaints generously | Get defensive or hide behind policy |

## 9. Brand integrity checklist

Before anything customer-facing ships, confirm:

- [ ] Every factual claim (grade, spec, price, sourcing) is **true and verifiable**.
- [ ] Certification is present/linked where a stone is shown.
- [ ] Pricing is transparent — no manipulative discounting.
- [ ] Voice matches [Voice & Tone](voice-and-tone.md).
- [ ] Visuals match [Visual Identity](visual-identity.md).
- [ ] The tagline, if used, is exact and in order.
- [ ] Tone fits the moment (celebratory, reassuring, or apologetic as appropriate).
- [ ] Nothing pressures the customer or manufactures urgency.

## 10. Governance

The Marketing & Brand team owns these guidelines. Proposed changes to brand promise, pillars, tagline, or naming conventions require sign-off from the Office of the Founder / CEO, because they touch the core of what customers trust us for.
