## 1. Positioning statement

> **For engaged couples and quality-conscious fine-jewelry buyers who refuse to choose between beauty, honesty, and price, Palencia Diamonds is the online fine jeweler that sells independently certified diamonds and handcrafted jewelry at transparent, defensible prices — because we believe the most meaningful purchases deserve the least amount of doubt.**

Unlike big-box chains that mark up mystery, and unlike anonymous online marketplaces that sell on price alone, Palencia pairs GIA/IGI certification and real craftsmanship with pricing a customer can actually verify — and service that lasts the life of the piece.

## 2. Category & frame of reference

- **Category:** Online-first fine diamond jewelry — engagement rings, wedding bands, and certified diamond pieces.
- **Frame of reference:** When a customer is choosing where to buy a diamond, they compare us against mall jewelry chains, luxury/heritage houses, and other online diamond retailers. That is the set we position within.
- **The customer's real question:** *"How do I spend a lot of money on something I can't fully evaluate, and not get taken advantage of?"* Our entire position is the answer to that question: **certification removes the guesswork, honest pricing removes the fear, and craftsmanship removes the compromise.**

## 3. Target customer segments

| Segment | Who they are | What they want | How we win them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Engaged couples** | First-time major jewelry buyers, often anxious, often on a budget that matters | To get it right, not overpay, and feel proud of the choice | Certification they can verify, transparent pricing, zero-pressure guidance, custom design |
| **Self-purchasers** | Confident buyers treating themselves (often women buying for themselves) | Quality and value on their own terms, no condescension | Honest specs, elegant everyday and statement pieces, respect and expertise |
| **Gift buyers** | Buying for a partner, parent, child, or milestone | Confidence they chose well, easy process, safety net | Guidance, generous returns/resizing, certification as reassurance |
| **Repeat & referral buyers** | Past customers and the people they send us | A repeat of the trust they already experienced | Consistency, lifetime service, remembering them |

## 4. Points of difference

What we have that competitors structurally cannot easily copy:

1. **Certification, always — and made verifiable.** Every center stone ships with an independent GIA or IGI report, presented plainly. We don't grade our own diamonds and ask you to trust us.
2. **Honest, defensible pricing.** Transparent prices without the markup-then-discount theater that defines mall retail. A customer can compare us line-by-line and understand exactly what they're paying for.
3. **Genuine craftsmanship.** Hand-set, individually inspected pieces — not mass-stamped inventory. Every piece passes [Quality Control](../03-operations/quality-control.md) before it ships.
4. **Confidence built into the purchase.** Free insured shipping with signature confirmation, complimentary resizing where the design allows, and manufacturing-defect coverage.
5. **Made yours.** Custom design with real choice of material, stone, and carat — accessible, not a luxury-house privilege.
6. **Responsible sourcing as a requirement.** Conflict-free, ethically sourced stones backed by real due diligence (Kimberley Process, supplier warranties). See [Responsible Sourcing](../05-compliance-ethics/responsible-sourcing.md).

| **Local independent jewelers** | Personal relationships, custom skill | Limited selection, inconsistent pricing, limited reach | Online convenience + selection + consistent standards |

## 6. Value proposition

**The Palencia trade:** the certainty of a certified, ethically sourced, handcrafted diamond — at a price you can verify and service that outlasts the sale — without the intimidation of a luxury house or the guesswork of a marketplace.

| Customer fear | How we remove it |
|---|---|
| "Am I overpaying?" | Transparent, defensible pricing |
| "Is the stone really what they say?" | Independent GIA/IGI certification, verifiable |
| "Is it well made?" | Hand-set, QC-inspected craftsmanship |
| "What if something's wrong?" | Insured shipping, resizing, defect coverage, generous service |
| "Where did this diamond come from?" | Documented responsible sourcing |

## 7. Brand pillars

The four pillars that every brand expression rests on (a subset of our strategic pillars, expressed for the customer):

1. **Certified** — independent proof, always.
2. **Handcrafted** — made by people who care, inspected before it ships.
3. **Honest** — pricing and grades you can trust and verify.
4. **Yours** — custom choice and lifetime service.

These map directly to the brand promise: *"Certified diamonds. Handcrafted jewelry. Honest prices."*

## 9. Messaging guardrails

- Lead with **trust and proof**, not price. We are honest about price; we do not compete as the cheapest.
- Never disparage competitors by name. We win by contrast, not attack.
- Every claim must be **true and verifiable** — certification, sourcing, service. See [Voice & Tone](voice-and-tone.md) for how we talk about diamonds honestly.
- Keep the emotional weight: these are life's biggest moments, not transactions.
