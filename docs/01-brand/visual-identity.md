## 1. What this document is

This is the visual system for Palencia Diamonds. It governs everything a customer sees — the site, product imagery, packaging, email, and certificate inserts — so the brand looks as trustworthy and precise as the diamonds we sell. Pair it with [Voice & Tone](voice-and-tone.md) (how we sound) and [Brand Guidelines](brand-guidelines.md) (the brand system overview).

**Guiding idea:** our visuals should feel *clear, precise, and honest* — the visual equivalent of handing someone the certificate. Restraint reads as confidence. Clutter reads as a chain store.

## 5. Photography & imagery

Imagery is where trust is visually earned. Our photography must be *accurate* — a customer should not be surprised when the piece arrives.

### Diamond & product photography
| Attribute | Standard |
|---|---|
| **Accuracy** | The stone must look like the stone. No misleading retouching of color, clarity, or size. |
| **Lighting** | Controlled, even lighting that shows true color and real brilliance/fire; no artificial sparkle added in post |
| **Backgrounds** | Clean neutral canvas (off-white/soft gray) for product shots; consistent across the catalog |
| **Scale** | Include scale cues (on-hand, macro with reference) so carat size is honestly represented |
| **Detail** | Macro shots that show craftsmanship — the setting, prongs, hand-finishing |
| **Certificate** | Where possible, show or link the actual GIA/IGI report alongside the stone |
| **Consistency** | Same angles, distances, and lighting setup across a collection so pieces compare fairly |

### Lifestyle photography
| Attribute | Standard |
|---|---|
| **Feel** | Real, warm, human moments — the meaning of the piece, not staged opulence |
| **Diversity** | Represent the range of real customers (see [Brand Positioning](brand-positioning.md) segments) with genuine inclusion |
| **Restraint** | Understated over flashy; the moment is the hero, the jewelry is the detail |
| **Consent** | Real customers only with documented consent |

### Imagery don'ts
- [ ] Don't over-retouch stones to imply a higher grade or larger size.
- [ ] Don't add fake sparkle, lens flares, or manipulated brilliance.
- [ ] Don't use inconsistent lighting that makes stones look different than they are.
- [ ] Don't use stock imagery that misrepresents our actual pieces.

## 7. Layout & design principles

- **Whitespace is a feature.** Generous spacing signals confidence and lets product breathe.
- **Grid & alignment.** Consistent grid; specs and prices align cleanly in tables.
- **No dark patterns.** No fake countdowns, forced urgency, pre-checked upsells, or hidden costs. The layout should make honest evaluation easy.
- **Certification-forward.** Certificates and specs are easy to find, not buried.

## 8. Accessibility

Accessibility is part of honesty — everyone should be able to evaluate a purchase clearly.

| Requirement | Standard |
|---|---|
| **Color contrast** | Meet WCAG 2.1 AA: ≥ 4.5:1 for body text, ≥ 3:1 for large text and meaningful UI elements. Verify final palette. |
| **Don't rely on color alone** | Pair color with text/icon for status (e.g., "Shipped" label, not just green) |
| **Alt text** | Every product and certificate image has descriptive alt text including key specs |
| **Type size** | Body text minimum `TODO: [16px web baseline]`; support browser zoom/reflow |
| **Focus states** | Visible keyboard focus on all interactive elements |
| **Motion** | Respect reduced-motion preferences; no essential info conveyed only by animation |
| **Forms** | Labels, error messages, and instructions that are clear and programmatically associated |
