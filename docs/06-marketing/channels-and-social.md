## 1. Purpose & how to use this

This document turns the [Marketing Strategy](marketing-strategy.md) into concrete, per-channel plays. Each channel entry follows the same structure:

- **Role in the funnel** — what job it does (create demand, capture it, nurture, or convert to advocacy).
- **Content type** — what actually gets made.
- **Cadence** — how often we show up.
- **KPIs** — how we know it's working.
- **Do's & Don'ts** — the guardrails that protect trust and brand.

Every channel expresses one identity — see [Brand Guidelines](../01-brand/brand-guidelines.md) — and hands warm leads to the owned relationship engine in [CRM & Clienteling](../04-sales-cx/crm-and-clienteling.md).

## 3. Organic social

### 3.1 Instagram — the brand's storefront window

- **Role in funnel:** Awareness and consideration. The place buyers "check if we're real" and fall for the pieces.
- **Content type:** Reels of pieces in natural light and on-hand; behind-the-craft (hand-setting, QC); certification explainers; customer moments (with consent); styling; carousels teaching the 4Cs; Stories for polls, Q&A, and shipping-deadline reminders.
- **Cadence:** `[4–6 feed/Reels per week + daily Stories] TODO:` confirm against capacity.
- **KPIs:** Reach & new-follower growth, save/share rate (intent signals), profile→site clicks, Story reply/DM volume.
- **Do's:** Show the actual certificate and craftsmanship; use real customers and real hands; reply to every DM like a concierge; keep captions warm, plain, and honest.
- **Don'ts:** No stock-photo perfection that misrepresents a piece; no invented scarcity ("only 2 left!"); no engagement-bait; never imply a grade we can't document.
- **Deep dive:** [Instagram Playbook](playbooks/instagram.md).

### 3.2 Pinterest — the quiet high-intent workhorse

- **Role in funnel:** Discovery/consideration. Pinterest users are *planning* — ring inspiration, wedding boards — which makes them unusually high-intent and long-cycle-friendly.
- **Content type:** Vertical pins of rings/styles linked to category and guide pages; "engagement ring styles," "how to choose," and gift-guide pins; rich pins pulling live product data.
- **Cadence:** `[Steady weekly pinning + seasonal boards ramped 8–12 weeks early] TODO:`.
- **KPIs:** Outbound clicks to site, saves, assisted conversions, impressions on priority styles.
- **Do's:** Link every pin to a genuinely useful destination; optimize pin titles/descriptions for search (Pinterest is a search engine); align with [Content & SEO](content-and-seo.md) topic clusters.
- **Don'ts:** Don't treat it as an ad dump; don't link to dead ends; don't neglect it — it compounds.

### 3.3 TikTok — education and personality

- **Role in funnel:** Awareness. Reach younger ring shoppers with education and honesty that cuts through jewelry-industry mystique.
- **Content type:** Short myth-busting/education ("what actually affects a diamond's price," "GIA vs IGI in 30s"), behind-the-craft, honest price breakdowns, trend reactions, founder/expert POV.
- **Cadence:** `[3–5 per week, trend-responsive] TODO:`.
- **KPIs:** Views, watch-through/completion, follows, saves, comment sentiment.
- **Do's:** Lead with a hook and teach something real; be transparent (honesty *is* the differentiator and it performs); use captions/subtitles.
- **Don'ts:** Don't chase trends that clash with a trust brand; don't overproduce into feeling like an ad; don't make claims you can't back.
- **Deep dive:** [TikTok Playbook](playbooks/tiktok.md).

### 3.4 Facebook - the trust check

- **Role in funnel:** Consideration. Gift buyers, older self-purchasers, and the family a ring buyer consults still vet a jeweler here; the page is a legitimacy check more than a reach channel. It is also home base for the Meta Ads system.
- **Content type:** Cross-posted best-of Instagram plus Facebook-native proof (customer stories, milestones, education); reviews/recommendations actively answered; Messenger at concierge standard.
- **Cadence:** `[2-3 posts/week] TODO:` confirm.
- **KPIs:** Review volume and rating, response time, page-driven assisted conversions.
- **Deep dive:** [Facebook & Meta Ads Playbook](playbooks/facebook.md).

### 3.5 YouTube - the research library

- **Role in funnel:** Consideration. Long-form education wins the weeks-long research phase; the second-largest search engine for buyer questions ("GIA vs IGI," "how much to spend").
- **Content type:** 8-15 min search-first guides, Shorts cut from long-form, craft and certification proof films, customer stories.
- **Cadence:** `[2 long-form/month + 2-3 Shorts/week] TODO:` confirm capacity.
- **KPIs:** Watch time and retention, views from search, guide downloads, assisted conversions and branded-search lift.
- **Deep dive:** [YouTube Playbook](playbooks/youtube.md).

## 4. Paid media

### 4.1 Paid search — capture the ready-to-buy

- **Role in funnel:** Purchase/capture. Meets buyers at "1 carat platinum solitaire" intent.
- **Content type:** Text ads on high-intent non-brand terms (styles, shapes, metals, occasions) + defensive brand terms; sitelinks to guides, reviews, financing, and policies.
- **Cadence:** Always-on; budgets ramp 2–4 weeks before seasonal peaks.
- **KPIs:** Conversion rate, ROAS, CAC/payback, impression share on priority terms.
- **Do's:** Bid to protect brand terms; send high-intent clicks to matching category/product pages; feature honest price, certification, and free insured shipping in copy; use negatives aggressively.
- **Don'ts:** Don't send ad clicks to the homepage; don't advertise stock we can't fulfill; don't overspend on broad informational terms better served by [Content & SEO](content-and-seo.md).
- **Deep dive:** [Google Ads Playbook](playbooks/google-ads.md).

### 4.2 Shopping / product listing ads — the visual capture layer

- **Role in funnel:** Purchase/capture. Image + price at the moment of comparison.
- **Content type:** Product feed with clean titles, accurate specs (4Cs, metal, carat), honest pricing, ratings, and strong imagery.
- **Cadence:** Always-on; feed hygiene continuous; budgets follow seasonality.
- **KPIs:** ROAS, CAC, impression share, feed disapproval rate (keep near zero).
- **Do's:** Keep the feed accurate and rich; include GIA/IGI and service terms where allowed; segment by margin/intent.
- **Don'ts:** Don't run a stale or inaccurate feed; don't ignore disapprovals; don't misrepresent price/availability.
- **Deep dive:** [Google Ads Playbook](playbooks/google-ads.md#5-merchant-center--the-shopping-feed).

### 4.3 Paid social — create demand & retarget

- **Role in funnel:** Awareness (prospecting) + consideration (retargeting the long cycle).
- **Content type:** Prospecting: education, craft, and hero-piece video/creative to lookalike & interest audiences. Retargeting: reminders, reviews, certification proof, and gentle deadline nudges to site visitors and email non-openers.
- **Cadence:** Always-on with creative refresh every `[2–4 weeks] TODO:` to fight fatigue; concentrated in seasonal peaks.
- **KPIs:** CPA/CAC, ROAS, view-through, creative fatigue (frequency, CTR decay).
- **Do's:** Separate prospecting vs retargeting budgets; refresh creative often; retarget with *trust* assets (reviews, guarantees), not just discounts.
- **Don'ts:** Don't blast one audience; don't rely on discounting a premium-trust brand; don't retarget people who already bought the same item.
- **Deep dive:** [Facebook & Meta Ads Playbook](playbooks/facebook.md) (Meta), [TikTok Playbook](playbooks/tiktok.md#6-tiktok-advertising) (TikTok), [Google Ads Playbook](playbooks/google-ads.md#7-demand-gen--youtube-campaigns) (Demand Gen/YouTube).

## 5. Owned lifecycle: email & SMS

Owned channels are the highest-ROI engine for a months-long buying cycle — full lifecycle design and clienteling live in [CRM & Clienteling](../04-sales-cx/crm-and-clienteling.md); this section covers the marketing-facing plays.

### 5.1 Email — the nurture backbone

- **Role in funnel:** Nurture (consideration) + retain/advocacy.
- **Content type:** Welcome/education series (4Cs, buying guide), browse/cart/abandon recovery, post-purchase care, review requests, referral invites, seasonal campaigns, VIP/anniversary reminders.
- **Cadence:** Trigger-based flows always-on; `[1–2 campaign sends/week] TODO:`, ramped seasonally.
- **KPIs:** Email capture rate (**≥ 5%** of new visitors), revenue per email, flow conversion, list growth, deliverability.
- **Do's:** Capture the email early with a genuinely useful guide; segment by intent and stage; lead flows with education and trust before offers; honor shipping-deadline reminders.
- **Don'ts:** Don't over-mail or buy lists; don't lead every email with a discount; don't let deliverability rot with unengaged sends.

### 5.2 SMS — timely, sparing, consent-first

- **Role in funnel:** Nurture + urgency (deadlines, back-in-stock, order/shipping updates).
- **Content type:** Explicit-opt-in only: shipping-deadline countdowns, order/delivery updates, VIP early access, restock alerts.
- **Cadence:** Sparing — `[≤ 4 marketing texts/month] TODO:`; transactional as needed.
- **KPIs:** Opt-in rate, CTR, revenue per send, unsubscribe rate (keep low).
- **Do's:** Get clear consent; keep it short, useful, and time-sensitive; always allow easy opt-out; comply with messaging regulations `TODO: confirm jurisdictional rules with counsel`.
- **Don'ts:** Don't text without consent; don't over-message; don't use SMS for long education.

## 6. Influencer & partnerships — borrowed trust

- **Role in funnel:** Awareness + trust. A credible voice vouching for a high-trust purchase shortcuts skepticism.
- **Content type:** Authentic reviews/unboxings, proposal stories (with consent), education collaborations, gifting features; partnerships with wedding/bridal and lifestyle creators or complementary brands (photographers, planners) `TODO: define partner tiers`.
- **Cadence:** Campaign-based, weighted to engagement and gifting seasons.
- **KPIs:** Reach, engagement rate, promo/referral-code attribution, assisted conversions, cost per engaged view.
- **Do's:** Vet for genuine fit and audience trust over follower count; require honest, disclosed (#ad) content; brief them on certification and honest-pricing story; prefer long-term ambassadors over one-offs.
- **Don'ts:** Don't buy fake reach or undisclosed endorsements (illegal and off-brand); don't script inauthentic hype; don't partner where values conflict (e.g., unethical sourcing claims).

## 7. Reviews & UGC — the trust engine

- **Role in funnel:** Trust at every stage; decisive at purchase.
- **Content type:** Verified product & site reviews, star ratings (feeding Product/Review schema — see [Content & SEO](content-and-seo.md)), customer photos/videos, proposal and "she said yes" stories (with consent), testimonials.
- **Cadence:** Continuous solicitation post-delivery (timed to receipt); UGC curated and re-shared weekly.
- **KPIs:** Review volume, average rating (**target ≥ 4.8★**), response rate to reviews, UGC volume & usage, conversion lift on pages with reviews.
- **Do's:** Ask every customer at the right post-delivery moment; respond to *every* review, especially critical ones, generously (a value: "earn trust, then earn it again"); showcase real customers with permission.
- **Don'ts:** Never fabricate, incentivize dishonestly, gate, or selectively suppress reviews — this violates our honesty value and platform/consumer-protection rules. A bad review handled well builds more trust than a perfect wall of praise.

## 8. Referral — advocacy as an acquisition channel

- **Role in funnel:** Advocacy → acquisition. Our vision metric is customers who "send us their family and friends" — referral operationalizes it.
- **Content type:** Give-get referral program (`TODO: define incentive & terms`), post-purchase referral invites, shareable links, wedding-party/gifting referrals.
- **Cadence:** Triggered after a delighted post-delivery moment; reinforced in email/SMS and clienteling.
- **KPIs:** Referral rate (% of customers who refer), referred-customer CAC (should be well below blended), referral-driven revenue share, referral→purchase conversion.
- **Do's:** Time the ask to peak delight (post-delivery, positive review); make sharing effortless; make the incentive feel generous and honest.
- **Don'ts:** Don't ask before value is delivered; don't make terms confusing; don't let referral rewards undercut price integrity.

## 9. Cross-channel operating rules

1. **One voice, many rooms.** Instagram, TikTok, email, and ads all sound like Palencia — warm, plain, precise, honest. See [Brand Guidelines](../01-brand/brand-guidelines.md).
2. **Create feeds capture.** Social/influencer/PR create demand; search/shopping capture it; email/CRM nurture and retain. Fund and measure them as a system, not rivals.
3. **Trust assets travel.** Certification proof, reviews, and guarantees belong in *every* channel, not just product pages.
4. **Consent and disclosure always.** Email/SMS opt-in, #ad disclosure, customer-content permission — non-negotiable.
5. **Seasonal concentration.** Ramp paid and campaign energy 2–4 weeks before peaks; ship content/SEO 8–12 weeks ahead (see [Marketing Strategy](marketing-strategy.md)).
6. **Hand off warm leads.** Any channel that captures interest routes to the owned nurture engine — see [CRM & Clienteling](../04-sales-cx/crm-and-clienteling.md).
