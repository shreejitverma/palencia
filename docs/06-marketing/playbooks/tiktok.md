## 1. Role in the funnel

TikTok is our **demand-creation and myth-busting** channel: it reaches younger ring shoppers years-to-months before purchase and plants the idea that a jeweler can be radically honest.

| Funnel stage | TikTok's job | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Reach the youngest ring-buyer cohort at scale | For You feed distribution, TikTok search |
| Consideration | Differentiate on honesty; pull viewers to owned depth | Education series, price-transparency content, profile link to guide |
| Purchase | Assist, not close | Retargeting via TikTok Ads; proof content |
| Advocacy | Real customer moments | Proposal stories, duets/stitches with customer content (with consent) |

TikTok rarely closes a five-figure sale on-platform.
Its job is to make Palencia the brand a buyer *already trusts* when they later search Google - judge it accordingly.

## 2. Why TikTok matters for a diamond brand

- The engaged-to-be segment's **youngest cohort researches here first**; "engagement ring" content has enormous native demand (ring reveals, proposal content, budget talk).
- TikTok is now a **search engine for Gen Z**: "how much to spend on engagement ring" is typed into TikTok search - captions and on-screen text are indexed.
- The platform rewards exactly our differentiator: **plain-spoken honesty cutting through industry mystique** performs, polished luxury advertising does not.
- Jewelry-industry myth-busting is a proven high-performing genre - and we can do it with real gemological authority.

## 4. Content strategy

### 4.1 Content pillars & series

Series > one-offs: recurring formats train both the algorithm and the audience.

| Series (pillar) | Format | Example episodes |
|---|---|---|
| **"Honest jeweler" myth-busting** | Talking-head + b-roll, 20-45s | "What actually affects a diamond's price"; "3 markup tricks to watch for"; "Is a bigger diamond always better? No - here's why" |
| **Price transparency** | Breakdown on camera | "What $5k really buys"; "Same carat, half the price - the difference"; "Where the money goes in a $10k ring" |
| **Certification in 30 seconds** | Explainer with a real report | "GIA vs IGI"; "How to read this certificate"; "Lab vs natural, honestly" |
| **Behind the bench** | Craft footage, ASMR-adjacent | Hand-setting, polishing, QC under the loupe |
| **Ring education** | Quick visual comparisons | Shapes on-hand, metals side-by-side, "hidden halo explained" |
| **Customer moments** | Story-led (with consent) | Proposal reactions, custom-build reveals, unboxings |

**Trend policy:** adapt trends only when they fit a trust brand (formats and sounds, yes; bait and drama, no).
Speed matters - trend windows are days, so the social owner can ship trend-fit content without a full review cycle, within the guardrails in §8.

### 4.2 The TikTok creative formula

- **Hook (0-1.5s):** name the fear or curiosity ("You're about to overpay for a diamond").
- **Payoff fast:** teach one true thing per video; one idea, not three.
- **Native production:** phone-shot, natural light, captions on, imperfect is fine - polish reads as advertising.
- **CTA soft:** "full guide in bio," "comment your budget and I'll tell you what it gets" - never hard-sell.
- **Cadence:** `[3-5/week] TODO:` confirm capacity; consistency beats bursts.

### 4.3 Cross-platform reuse

Every TikTok is a candidate for Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts (re-exported without watermark, re-captioned per platform).
Winning TikTok hooks feed the paid-creative pipeline on Meta (see [Facebook & Meta Ads Playbook §7](facebook.md#7-creative-playbook)).

## 5. Creators & collaboration

- Prioritize **wedding/proposal-adjacent creators and honest-review voices** over jewelry glam accounts; vet per [Channels & Social §6](../channels-and-social.md#6-influencer--partnerships--borrowed-trust).
- Use duets/stitches to respond to ring questions and misconceptions in the wild - free, authentic reach.
- **Spark Ads first:** boost the creator's (or our) organic post with authorization codes rather than making display-style ads; authenticity carries the performance.
- All partnerships disclosed with the platform's branded-content toggle plus plain-language disclosure.

## 6. TikTok advertising

Paid on TikTok scales what already works organically - never runs creative that couldn't survive the For You feed.

| Foundation / campaign | Standard |
|---|---|
| **Pixel + Events API** | Both installed, deduplicated; ViewContent, AddToCart, InitiateCheckout, Purchase, Lead; consent rules per [Data Privacy & Security](../../05-compliance-ethics/data-privacy-and-security.md) |
| **Prospecting (Spark Ads)** | Objective: traffic/lead early, conversions once signal exists; broad + interest (engagement, wedding planning) audiences; creative = top organic posts |
| **Lead capture** | Guide download as the optimization event - right-sized for a long cycle where on-platform purchase is rare |

## 8. Compliance & guardrails

- Every claim documentable - grades, prices, comparisons. Myth-busting must itself be rigorously true.
- No trend participation involving drama, bait, misleading stunts, or anything that cheapens a trust brand.
- Customer content and appearances require written consent; branded content always disclosed.
- Commercial-sound licensing: business accounts must use the commercial music library or licensed/original audio - trending consumer sounds are often not cleared for brands. `TODO:` confirm workflow.
- Comment sections moderated for impersonation and jewelry-scam replies (report and block).
- Contingency: TikTok faces recurring regulatory uncertainty in some markets - the audience strategy must keep feeding owned channels (email capture) so reach here is never solely rented. `TODO:` revisit exposure annually.

## 9. Do's & don'ts

**Do**
- Lead with a hook and teach one real thing per video.
- Keep one consistent, credible face on the account.
- Answer comments; turn questions into next week's videos.
- Boost winners with Spark Ads; let organic prove creative before paid scales it.

**Don't**
- Overproduce into looking like an ad.
- Chase every trend; chase the right ones fast.
- Make claims we can't back - the comment section will find out, publicly.
- Expect last-click sales; TikTok plants, Google and email harvest.
