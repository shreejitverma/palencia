## 1. Role in the funnel

Instagram is our **brand storefront window** and the platform where buyers "check if we're real."
For an engagement-ring buyer researching for weeks, the Instagram profile functions as a second homepage: proof of craft, proof of happy customers, proof we exist and can be trusted.

| Funnel stage | Instagram's job | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Get discovered by ring shoppers and gift buyers | Reels reach, Explore, hashtags/SEO, collabs |
| Consideration | Prove craft, certification, and honesty; capture the lead | Grid as portfolio, Stories education, DM concierge, link-in-bio guide download |
| Purchase | Remove last-mile doubt | Reviews/UGC highlights, product tags, DM-to-consult |
| Advocacy | Showcase real customers; invite sharing | Proposal features, tagged UGC, "she said yes" moments |

Paid placements on Instagram run through the shared Meta Ads architecture - see the [Facebook & Meta Ads Playbook](facebook.md) for account structure, targeting, and budgets.
This document owns Instagram-specific organic strategy and Instagram-first creative.

## 2. Why Instagram matters for a diamond brand

- Jewelry is a **visual, emotional category**; Instagram is the highest-intent visual discovery surface for style research after Pinterest.
- The 25-40 audience skews directly onto our **engaged-to-be and self-purchaser segments** (see [Marketing Strategy](../marketing-strategy.md#4-who-we-are-talking-to-audience-segments)).
- Buyers routinely **vet a jeweler's Instagram before purchasing** - an inactive or thin profile reads as risk on a five-figure purchase.
- Saves and shares are strong intent signals: a saved ring post is a bookmark in a months-long decision.

## 3. Profile foundations (the storefront)

The profile is optimized for the 5-second trust check a new visitor performs.

| Element | Standard |
|---|---|
| Handle & name field | `@palenciadiamonds`; name field includes searchable terms ("Palencia Diamonds \| Certified Engagement Rings") - the name field is indexed by Instagram search |
| Bio | Brand promise in plain words: certified (GIA/IGI), handcrafted, honest prices, free insured shipping. One clear CTA |
| Link in bio | Link hub to: buying guide download (email capture), best sellers, book a consult, reviews page. Track with UTMs |
| Highlights | Fixed set: Certification, Craft, Reviews, Custom Design, FAQ, Shipping & Returns. Refresh quarterly |
| Grid (first 9-12 posts) | Must always read as a portfolio: real pieces, real hands, real customers. Audit monthly |
| Shopping | Product tagging enabled on eligible posts; catalog synced from the product feed (see [Facebook & Meta Ads Playbook](facebook.md#5-catalog--feed)) |
| Contact & DM | Response templates for the top 20 questions; concierge tone per [Voice & Tone](../../01-brand/voice-and-tone.md) |

## 4. Content strategy

### 4.1 Content pillars & mix

Mapped to the brand pillars in the [Marketing Strategy](../marketing-strategy.md#3-positioning-driven-messaging).

| Pillar | Share of content | What it looks like on Instagram |
|---|---|---|
| **Craft & product** (Handcrafted) | ~40% | Reels of pieces in natural light and on-hand; macro sparkle shots; behind-the-bench setting and QC; before/after custom builds |
| **Education & honesty** (Certified, Honest) | ~25% | 4Cs carousels; "how to read a GIA report"; honest price breakdowns; "smaller but cleaner" trade-off content |
| **Customers & proof** | ~20% | Proposal stories (with consent); unboxings; review screenshots; tagged UGC reshares |
| **Brand & culture** | ~10% | Team, founder POV, sourcing ethics, packaging, care rituals |
| **Seasonal & campaign** | ~5% | Gift guides, shipping-deadline countdowns, seasonal edits |

> Ratios are planning guides, not quotas. Never publish filler to hit a mix.

### 4.3 Hooks that work for this category

Lead with the fear we relieve, then the proof (per [Marketing Strategy](../marketing-strategy.md#3-positioning-driven-messaging)):

- "What a $[X] engagement ring actually gets you"
- "How to tell if a diamond is really the grade they claim"
- "3 things jewelers hope you never learn about pricing"
- "We hand-set every stone. Here's what that means" (bench footage)
- "GIA vs IGI in 30 seconds"
- "The moment she said yes" (customer story, with consent)

### 4.4 Discovery: hashtags, SEO, and geography

- Instagram search is increasingly **keyword-driven**: write captions that state the subject plainly ("oval halo engagement ring in platinum"), not just poetically.
- Hashtags: 3-8 targeted tags per post mixing category (#engagementring), style (#ovalring, #haloring), and intent (#proposalideas); skip generic mega-tags that attract bots.
- Alt text on every post: describe the piece precisely - accessibility and search in one move.

## 5. DM concierge & lead handling

DMs are a sales channel, not a support queue.
A ring buyer who DMs is deep in consideration.

- **Response SLA:** `[< 2 business hours during working hours] TODO:` confirm staffing.
- Every DM answered like a concierge: warm, precise, zero pressure - see [Sales Playbook](../../04-sales-cx/sales-playbook.md).
- Standard paths: style question → send 2-3 relevant pieces + guide link; budget question → honest budget framing + consult offer; certification question → explain and show a sample report.
- Escalate high-intent conversations (budget stated, timeline stated) to a named consultant and log in CRM - see [CRM & Clienteling](../../04-sales-cx/crm-and-clienteling.md).
- Story replies and comment DMs get the same standard; consider keyword auto-replies ("GUIDE" → buying-guide link) where they stay honest and helpful.

## 6. Influencer & collab mechanics on Instagram

Strategy and vetting live in [Channels & Social §6](../channels-and-social.md#6-influencer--partnerships--borrowed-trust); Instagram-specific mechanics:

- **Collab posts** (co-authored posts) with creators put our content in both audiences' feeds with shared engagement - prefer these over mention-only posts.
- **Partnership ads (whitelisting):** run paid behind top-performing creator content with the paid-partnership label; requires creator approval in advance - see [Facebook & Meta Ads Playbook](facebook.md).
- Every partnership uses the **paid-partnership label** and honest disclosure - non-negotiable.
- Brief creators on certification and honest pricing; never script fake enthusiasm.

## 7. Paid on Instagram (summary)

Full architecture, audiences, budgets, and measurement live in the [Facebook & Meta Ads Playbook](facebook.md).
Instagram-specific rules:

- **Placements:** Reels and Stories are the primary Instagram placements; creative must be built 9:16 native, not cropped from feed assets.
- **Creative source:** the best-performing organic Reels graduate into paid; boost proof-heavy content (reviews, certification explainers) for retargeting.
- **Retargeting:** engagers (profile visitors, video viewers, saver audiences) enter the consideration retargeting pool alongside site visitors.

## 10. Compliance & guardrails

- **No invented scarcity, no engagement bait, no pressure** - trust is the product.
- Never state or imply a grade we cannot document; show the certificate when making certification claims.
- Customer content (proposals, faces, names) requires **written consent** before reposting - see [Data Privacy & Security](../../05-compliance-ethics/data-privacy-and-security.md).
- All paid partnerships disclosed with the platform's paid-partnership tools plus plain-language disclosure.
- Giveaways/contests (if run) must follow platform promotion rules and local law; `TODO:` legal review before the first one.
- Follow [Brand Guidelines](../../01-brand/brand-guidelines.md) and [Visual Identity](../../01-brand/visual-identity.md) in every asset; imagery must represent the actual product.

## 11. Do's & don'ts

**Do**
- Show real pieces, real hands, real light, real customers.
- Teach something true in every educational post.
- Reply to every DM and genuine comment like a concierge.
- Let saves and DMs - not likes - define success.

**Don't**
- Post stock-perfect renders that misrepresent a piece.
- Chase trends that clash with a trust brand.
- Buy followers or engagement - ever.
- Lead with discounts; lead with proof.
