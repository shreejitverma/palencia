## 1. Role in the funnel

YouTube is where high-consideration research happens in depth.
A buyer who will spend five figures watches "how to buy a diamond" videos the way they would read a book - and the channel that teaches them honestly earns the sale.

| Funnel stage | YouTube's job | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Reach researchers at the start of the cycle | Search-ranked education, Shorts, suggested videos |
| Consideration | Become the trusted teacher; capture the email | Deep guides, comparisons, honest pricing breakdowns; description links to guide download |
| Purchase | Prove craft and process; remove fear | Bench/craft films, certification walk-throughs, customer stories |
| Advocacy | Showcase real outcomes | Proposal films, testimonial videos (with consent) |

YouTube is also the **second-largest search engine**: video SEO here compounds exactly like web SEO in [Content & SEO](../content-and-seo.md), and the two share one topic-cluster plan.

## 2. Why YouTube matters for a diamond brand

- **Long-form matches long consideration.** A 12-minute "4Cs explained" video builds more trust than fifty feed posts; watch time is trust time.
- **Search intent is explicit:** "GIA vs IGI," "how much to spend on an engagement ring," "lab vs natural diamond" - buyers type their fears directly into YouTube.
- **Evergreen compounding:** a ranked education video earns qualified views for years at zero marginal cost.
- **Proof format:** video is the only medium that can show hand-setting, sparkle in natural light, and a real certificate at once.

## 3. Channel foundations

| Element | Standard |
|---|---|
| Channel identity | Name, handle, banner, and about section state the brand promise; links to buying guide (email capture), site, and reviews |

| End screens & cards | Every video routes to the next step in its cluster + one soft product/guide CTA |

| Captions | Accurate captions on every video (accessibility + search indexing) |
| Comments | Answered like a concierge; question mining feeds the content backlog |

## 4. Content strategy

### 4.1 The three content engines

| Engine | Format | Job | Cadence (planning) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Search-first education** | 8-15 min long-form | Rank for buyer questions; win the research phase | `[2/month] TODO:` confirm capacity |
| **Shorts** | < 60s vertical | Discovery; feed new viewers into long-form | `[2-3/week]` - cut from long-form + TikTok/Reels adaptations |
| **Proof films** | 3-8 min | Craft, certification, customer stories | `[1/month]` |

### 4.2 Priority long-form topics (mapped to search demand)

| Cluster | Example videos | Intent |
|---|---|---|
| 4Cs & certification | "The 4Cs explained by a gemologist"; "How to read a GIA report"; "GIA vs IGI: what actually differs"; "Lab-grown vs natural: the honest comparison" | Informational |
| Budget & honesty | "How much should you spend on an engagement ring? (Real numbers)"; "What a $5,000 / $10,000 / $20,000 ring gets you"; "Why two 1-carat diamonds can differ 2x in price" | Commercial |
| Styles & settings | "Solitaire vs halo vs three-stone"; "Platinum vs white gold"; "Choosing a diamond shape for her hand" | Commercial |
| Craft & custom | "How we hand-set a diamond (full process)"; "A custom ring from sketch to proposal" | Consideration/proof |
| Care & ownership | "How to clean your ring"; "Insurance and appraisals explained" | Retention |

**Content rules:** teach the honest version, including trade-offs ("when a smaller, cleaner stone beats a bigger one") - the honesty is the differentiation, per [Marketing Strategy §3](../marketing-strategy.md#3-positioning-driven-messaging).

### 4.3 Video SEO checklist (every upload)

- [ ] One primary query per video; title states it plainly (+ a reason to watch), <= ~60 chars visible.
- [ ] First 15 seconds confirm the payoff (retention cliff lives here).
- [ ] Chapters/timestamps for every section (search + UX).
- [ ] Description: payoff, chapters, guide link with UTM, cluster links.
- [ ] Accurate captions uploaded or verified.
- [ ] Custom thumbnail per house style; honest to content.
- [ ] End screen → next cluster video + guide CTA; cards at relevant moments.
- [ ] Added to the correct playlist; cross-linked from the matching web article ([Content & SEO](../content-and-seo.md)) with VideoObject schema on the page.

## 5. YouTube advertising

YouTube ads run from the Google Ads account - shared architecture, conversion tracking, and budgets live in the [Google Ads Playbook](google-ads.md).
YouTube-specific strategy:

| Campaign | Format | Audience | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| **In-market prospecting** | Skippable in-stream + in-feed | In-market: engagement rings, fine jewelry; life events: recently engaged; custom segments from ring-related searches | Reach active researchers with education-led creative |
| **Remarketing** | Skippable in-stream, Shorts placements | Site visitors, channel viewers/subscribers, YouTube engagers (excl. purchasers) | Sequence proof: reviews → certification → consult offer |
| **Demand Gen support** | Video across YouTube/Discover/Gmail | Lookalike-style and intent signals | Feed the long cycle; see [Google Ads Playbook §7](google-ads.md#7-demand-gen--youtube-campaigns) |

**Creative rules for video ads**

- Front-load the value: the first 5 seconds must state who we are and the fear we relieve ("Buying a diamond and scared of overpaying?") - assume the skip.
- Education-led ads outperform product-spin ads in this category; repurpose the strongest organic openings.
- Always include captions; design for sound-off.
- Frequency caps on remarketing; persistence never becomes pressure.

## 7. Production & operating rhythm

| Cadence | Activity |
|---|---|
| Weekly | Publish per calendar; answer comments; clip Shorts from recent long-form |
| Monthly | Retention-curve review per video (find and fix drop-off patterns); topic backlog refresh from comments, DMs, and search data |
| Quarterly | Cluster performance review; refresh titles/thumbnails on decaying evergreen videos; seasonal content shipped 8-12 weeks ahead (see [Marketing Strategy §8](../marketing-strategy.md#8-seasonality--the-marketing-calendar)) |

**Production standards:** natural light, real pieces, real people; on-camera expertise (gemologist/founder) beats anonymous voiceover; accuracy checked against [Diamond Education: The 4Cs](../../09-product/diamond-education-4cs.md) before publishing.

## 8. Compliance & guardrails

- Every factual claim (grades, prices, sourcing) documentable; certification shown when claimed.
- Customer appearances require written consent - see [Data Privacy & Security](../../05-compliance-ethics/data-privacy-and-security.md).
- Sponsored or gifted-creator content disclosed per FTC rules and platform tools.
- Music licensing verified for every video (no trending-audio shortcuts on YouTube).
- Comments moderated for spam/scams (fake "WhatsApp me" jewelry scammers target jewelry channels - remove and report).

## 9. Do's & don'ts

**Do**
- Answer the exact questions buyers type into YouTube, better and more honestly than anyone.
- Put a gemologist's face and name on the education - E-E-A-T applies to video.
- Cut every long-form video into Shorts; cross-link video and web content both ways.
- Ship seasonal videos 8-12 weeks early so they rank in time.

**Don't**
- Chase viral topics unrelated to buying jewelry.
- Use clickbait thumbnails that overpromise - retention and trust both die.
- Publish thin videos to hit cadence; one great guide beats four shallow ones.
- Judge YouTube on last-click conversions.
