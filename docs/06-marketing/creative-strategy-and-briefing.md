## 1. The Core Philosophy: Creative is the New Targeting

In modern algorithmic advertising (Meta Advantage+, TikTok Smart Performance, Google PMax), manual audience micro-targeting has been replaced by **Multimodal Semantic Creative Matching**.

- If your creative opens with a **macro shot of a 3-carat GIA-certified diamond on a jeweler’s loupe**, the algorithm serves the ad to users researching diamond grading and engagement ring specifications.
- If your creative opens with an **emotional couple proposal story in Lake Como**, the algorithm routes impressions to relationship milestone audiences.

Every piece of creative is an intentional audience filter. We do not design for generic virality; we engineer for **high-intent trust, risk reversal, and qualified conversions**.

## 5. Creator & UGC Briefing Template

When commissioning content creators, wedding influencers, or UGC partners, provide this standardized brief:

```markdown
# CREATOR BRIEF: PALENCIA DIAMONDS — AUTHENTIC UNBOXING & CRAFT REVIEW

### 1. Objective
Produce a 15–30 second high-retention vertical video (9:16) showcasing the unboxing, brilliance, and certification of a Palencia custom engagement ring/necklace.

### 2. Mandatory Visual Elements
- [ ] Natural window light or golden-hour outdoor light (NO harsh yellow artificial lighting).
- [ ] Clean, manicured hands wearing the piece.
- [ ] Crisp macro focus showing the sparkle and setting details.
- [ ] Visible GIA/IGI certificate card and luxury presentation box.

### 3. Audio & Dialogue Guardrails
- **Tone:** Warm, authentic, genuinely impressed, plain English.
- **Do Mention:** "Independent GIA/IGI certificate," "Hand-set craftsmanship," "Honest transparent pricing," "Free insured shipping."
- **Do NOT Mention:** Never use words like "cheap," "discount," "bargain," or make exaggerated grading claims.

### 4. Deliverables
- 1x Raw 4K 60fps MP4 master (no text burn-in).
- 1x Edited version with on-screen captions & hook text.
- 3x Alternate 3-second opening hook variations.
```
