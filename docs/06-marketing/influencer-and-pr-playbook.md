When loaning pieces valued over **$5,000** for photoshoots, weddings, or red carpet events, the following protocols are legally mandatory:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Security & Legal Clause              │ Protocol Standard                                      │
├──────────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ **Certificate of Insurance (COI)**   │ The recipient/production must provide a Certificate of │

├──────────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ **Insured Armored Transit**          │ Shipped exclusively via insured signature-required     │

```

## 4. FTC Disclosure & Compliance Guardrails

Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines mandate clear, conspicuous disclosures on all paid, gifted, or affiliate content:

- **Video Content (TikTok/Reels)**: Must feature `#ad` or `#PalenciaPartner` prominently on-screen in the first 3 seconds of the video, NOT buried in captions.
- **Audio Mentions**: Must verbally state *"Thanks to Palencia Diamonds for gifting this piece"* or *"Paid partnership with Palencia Diamonds."*
- **No Deceptive Claims**: Creators may never state or imply a diamond grade or origin that conflicts with the accompanying GIA/IGI certificate.
